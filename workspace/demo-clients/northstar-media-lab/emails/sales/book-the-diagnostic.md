# Book the diagnostic

**Trigger:** `audit.opened_no_call_24h` (Loops event)
**Segment:** Sales-qualified prospect
**Stage:** Sales
**Send timing:** 24 hours after audit open if no call is booked

## Subject Line
Primary: Want the 14-day test plan?
Variant A: We can map the first test
Variant B: Next step after the audit

## Preview Text
If the scorecard matched reality, we can turn it into a test plan.

## Body
You opened the audit, so I am going to keep this direct.

If the scorecard matched what you are seeing in the business, the next useful step is a 30-minute diagnostic call.

That call has one job: decide whether there is a clean 14-day Facebook ad test worth running.

We will cover:

1. The service line with the best short-term revenue case.
2. The ZIP cluster where you can actually serve demand.
3. The offer angle most likely to produce serious calls.
4. The follow-up step needed so leads do not sit untouched.

You will leave with a simple test plan even if we are not the right agency. If the account needs tracking work before more ad spend, we will say that. If the offer is not ready, we will say that too.

No long pitch. No mystery audit language. Just the next decision.

Before the call, open the audit and mark anything that feels off. We would rather correct the plan with your field context than pretend an outside read is perfect. The best test usually comes from combining account data with what your team hears on the phone.

If the timing is bad, book anyway for next week. A scheduled decision beats another month of unclear spend.

## CTA
Book the diagnostic call.
