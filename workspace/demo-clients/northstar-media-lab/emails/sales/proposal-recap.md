Here is the short version of the plan we mapped today.

Northstar would start with one clear test before expanding spend:

1. **Service line:** the offer with the best margin and fastest close cycle.
2. **Service area:** the ZIP cluster your team can serve quickly.
3. **Creative:** three angles, each tied to a different buyer belief.
4. **Lead handling:** callback timing and status tracking inside your CRM.
5. **Reporting:** weekly readout on booked calls, not just form fills.

The first 30 days are about finding a repeatable signal. We are not trying to prove every market, every offer, and every audience at once. That is how accounts become expensive without becoming clearer.

Your proposal includes setup, first-month management, reporting rhythm, and the access list we need to begin.

The next decision is simple: do you want us to build the first test and own the weekly readout?

If you approve, we will begin with tracking and access before we touch spend. That order matters. A clean first week gives us a baseline the owner, sales team, and ad account can all trust.

If you want one change before approval, reply with the exact concern. We can adjust the first test faster than we can guess what is blocking the yes.
