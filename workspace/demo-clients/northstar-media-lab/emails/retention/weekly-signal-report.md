## Body
This week's signal report is ready.

The report is organized around three questions:

1. What spent money?
2. What created qualified calls?
3. What should change next week?

For this demo account, Angle B created fewer form fills than Angle A, but it produced stronger call notes. That is the kind of signal we care about. Cheaper leads do not win if the team cannot reach them or they ask for work outside the service area.

Our recommended next move is to keep Angle B active, pause the weakest variant, and test a more direct opening line against the current winner.

Please review the report before Monday. If your sales team saw a different pattern on the phone, add that note. The ad account should learn from the real conversation, not only the form submission.

One weekly note from the field can change the next test. If a lead sounded serious but was outside the service area, that is targeting context. If several leads asked the same question, that is creative context. Add it before we rotate spend.

We will bring the numbers. You bring the sales reality. The report works when both sides are visible.

That is how next week's test gets sharper.
