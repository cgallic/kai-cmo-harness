# Know another owner?

**Trigger:** `client.milestone.90d` (Loops event)
**Segment:** Active client
**Stage:** Referral
**Send timing:** 90 days after client start if account health is green

## Subject Line
Primary: Know another owner?
Variant A: One quick favor
Variant B: Who else needs cleaner ads?

## Preview Text
If another owner is guessing on Facebook spend, send them our way.

## Body
Quick favor.

If you know another owner who is spending on Facebook ads but still guessing what the spend created, we would be grateful for an introduction.

The best fit is a local service company with:

1. A real monthly ad budget.
2. Someone who answers new leads quickly.
3. A service area they can define clearly.
4. Enough job value to support paid acquisition.

The wrong fit is someone looking for magic cheap leads with no follow-up process. We will politely pass on those.

You do not need to sell us. A simple forward is enough:

"Northstar helped us connect ad tests to booked-call quality. Worth a conversation if you are trying to clean up Facebook spend."

If they request an audit, we will send you a note so you know the introduction landed.

We protect referred owners by starting with the audit, not a retainer pitch. If the account is not ready, we will tell them what to fix first. That keeps the introduction useful even when the timing is not right.

A good introduction is usually one sentence. Send the owner our way if they want a clean read on whether Facebook can create booked calls for their service area.

## CTA
Forward this to one owner.
