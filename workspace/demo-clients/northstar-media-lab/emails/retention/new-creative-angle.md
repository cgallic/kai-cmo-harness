## Body
The next ad angle is ready for approval.

This one tests a different buyer belief:

**Current belief:** "I should wait until the problem gets worse."

**Test angle:** "Waiting usually turns a small repair into a bigger job."

That angle is useful because it does not rely on a discount. It gives the buyer a reason to act sooner while keeping the offer tied to your actual service economics.

We will run it against the current winner, not replace the account all at once. The goal is to learn whether urgency beats reassurance for this service line and ZIP cluster.

Please review the draft before noon tomorrow. If the language does not match how your team talks on the phone, add a note. Your sales language is often the best creative source.

We are looking for one of three answers: approve as written, approve with one edit, or reject because the angle is wrong. A fast no is better than a quiet maybe. Quiet maybes keep old ads running after the signal has faded.

If you reject it, include the phrase your team would use instead. Real sales language beats polished ad language almost every time.

That phrase may become the next headline.
