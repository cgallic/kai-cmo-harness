Thanks for requesting a Facebook Ads Waste Audit.

We are going to look at three things before anything else:

1. Where spend is leaking before a real buyer appears.
2. Which lead forms create callbacks versus dead-end names.
3. What your first 14-day creative test should prove.

Most local service ad accounts do not fail because the owner picked the wrong button. They fail because the account reports a lead while the sales team experiences a bad fit, a no-answer, or a job too far outside the service area.

That is the gap we check first.

You will get a short scorecard with the top three fixes ranked by urgency. If the account is already clean, we will say that too. The point is not to make a scary report. The point is to give you a clear next decision.

If you want the audit to be more useful, reply with the service area you care about most and the average booked job value. We will use those numbers as context.

Those two numbers change the read. A campaign that looks expensive for a $300 job can be sensible for a $6,000 install. Service area matters the same way. A cheap lead two counties away is not cheap.
