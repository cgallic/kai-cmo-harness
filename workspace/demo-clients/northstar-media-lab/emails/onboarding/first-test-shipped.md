## Body
Your first Facebook ad test is live.

This test is intentionally narrow:

1. One service line.
2. One service area.
3. Three creative angles.
4. One lead handling rule.

That narrow setup makes the first week easier to read. If we test too much at once, the report gets noisy and the next decision gets slower.

This week, we are watching the full path from impression to booked conversation. Cost per lead matters, but it is not the main answer. The stronger question is: which angle creates a buyer your team can actually reach and qualify?

You do not need to check the ad account every morning. If spend, lead quality, or tracking looks off, we will flag it. Your first weekly signal report arrives on Friday with the recommended next move.

For now, the most helpful thing your team can do is tag each new conversation accurately. Mark wrong area, bad fit, no answer, qualified, estimate booked, or sold. Those tags turn the first report into a decision tool.

Do not worry about perfect notes. Short, consistent labels are enough for week one. We need a pattern, not a novel.

That pattern decides Friday's next move with less debate.
