# Kickoff agenda

**Trigger:** `kickoff.scheduled` (Loops event)
**Segment:** New client
**Stage:** Onboarding
**Send timing:** 1 day before kickoff

## Subject Line
Primary: Tomorrow's kickoff agenda
Variant A: What we will decide tomorrow
Variant B: Kickoff: the 4 decisions

## Preview Text
The kickoff is built around four decisions, not a long slide tour.

## Body
Tomorrow's kickoff is built around four decisions.

1. Which service line gets the first 14-day test?
2. Which service area is in or out?
3. Which buyer belief should the first creative angle challenge?
4. What counts as a qualified lead for your team?

Bring any recent sales notes, call recordings, or examples of jobs you want more often. Those details will shape the first ad angle more than generic competitor research.

We will not spend the call touring every dashboard. You hired us to create signal, not more tabs.

By the end of kickoff, you should know:

- what goes live first,
- what we are measuring,
- who owns callback timing,
- and when the first weekly report arrives.

If there is one deal type you do not want, bring that too. Knowing what to avoid keeps the ad account from learning the wrong lesson.

The strongest kickoff notes are concrete. "More emergency HVAC calls within 20 miles" is useful. "Better leads" is too broad. The tighter the first definition, the faster we can judge whether Facebook is sending the right people.

Please also bring one example of a customer you wish you had more often. We will translate that example into targeting, form questions, and ad language.

## CTA
Add sales notes to the kickoff doc.
