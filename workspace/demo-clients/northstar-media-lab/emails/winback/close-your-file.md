# Should we close your file?

**Trigger:** `prospect.inactive_30d` (Loops event)
**Segment:** Inactive prospect
**Stage:** Win-back
**Send timing:** 30 days after last meaningful prospect activity

## Subject Line
Primary: Should we close your file?
Variant A: Still want the ad plan?
Variant B: Close this out?

## Preview Text
If Facebook ads are still on the table, we can reopen the plan.

## Body
Should we close your file?

You requested an audit, reviewed the plan, and then the conversation went quiet. That is normal. Owners get pulled back into the business.

Before we archive the notes, I wanted to ask one clear question:

Are Facebook ads still a priority for this quarter?

If yes, we can reopen the plan and adjust it around what changed. Maybe the service area shifted. Maybe staffing changed. Maybe you need to fix follow-up before buying more traffic.

If no, no problem. We will close the file and stop the follow-up.

Either answer helps. The only bad outcome is leaving an old plan half-open while the business has moved on.

If the answer is yes, send one sentence about what changed since the audit. New market, new staff, slower phones, higher lead costs, or a different offer all count. We can update the plan around that change instead of restarting from scratch.

If the answer is no, reply no and we will close the loop.

We ask because old ad plans decay quickly. A service area changes, competitors copy offers, staff capacity shifts, and call handling improves or breaks. A plan from last month may need a different first test today.

## CTA
Reply yes or no.
