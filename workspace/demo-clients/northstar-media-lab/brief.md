# Email System Brief

## Context
Northstar Media Lab is a demo Facebook ads agency used to showcase Kai CMO Harness. The goal is to prove how Kai turns a loose business idea into a usable lifecycle email system with mapped triggers, segments, example templates, quality checks, and implementation notes.

## Audience
- Owner-operators of local service companies
- Marketing managers at multi-location service brands

## Format
- Loops-ready lifecycle and transactional email templates
- A practical map that shows stage, trigger, priority, and segment
- A presentation deck for explaining the system live

## Primary Offer
Free Facebook Ads Waste Audit.

## Core Message
Facebook ads fail for local services when agencies optimize for form fills instead of booked calls. Northstar gives owners a weekly decision rhythm: what spent money, what booked calls, what gets tested next.

## System Goal
Move a prospect from audit request to booked diagnostic call, signed proposal, clean onboarding, weekly reporting, referral, and win-back.

## Demo Assumption
All numbers are labeled as demo examples. No client-facing claims are factual unless tied to a source inside the demo folder.
