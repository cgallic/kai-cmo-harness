# MARKETING.md — Demo Client Brand Bible

## Product
- **Name:** Northstar Media Lab
- **One-liner:** Northstar Media Lab runs Facebook and Instagram ad programs for local service companies that need booked calls, not prettier dashboards.
- **Category:** Paid social advertising agency
- **URL:** Demo brand, no live website

## ICP

### Primary ICP
- **Title/Role:** Owner, general manager, or growth lead
- **Company size:** 8-75 employees, $1M-$12M annual revenue
- **Industry:** HVAC, roofing, plumbing, dental, med spa, legal, and home services
- **Budget authority:** Yes; owner approves ad spend and agency fee
- **Trigger event:** Lead flow drops, referrals slow, Google Ads gets expensive, or a competitor starts filling the local market with Meta ads
- **Current method:** Boosted posts, basic lead forms, outsourced ad account with weak reporting, or no paid social program
- **Pain severity:** 8/10

### Secondary ICP
- **Title/Role:** Marketing manager at a multi-location local service brand
- **Company size:** 50-250 employees
- **Industry:** Multi-location healthcare, home services, wellness, legal
- **Budget authority:** Influences spend; owner, partner, or VP approves
- **Trigger event:** Expansion into a new service area or a failed in-house test
- **Current method:** Internal generalist runs ads without creative testing rhythm
- **Pain severity:** 7/10

### Anti-ICP
- Tiny local shops with no sales follow-up, no monthly ad budget, or no ability to answer calls quickly.

## Personas

### Persona 1: The Owner-Operator — based on Shock Absorber
- **Who they are:** Owner or GM stuck between revenue targets, staff pressure, and unpredictable lead flow.
- **Core frustration:** They are accountable for growth but cannot tell which marketing activity caused the next booked job.
- **Language they use:** "We need the phone ringing," "I do not need another report," "What did we actually get from this spend?"
- **Objections:** "Facebook leads are low quality"; "we tried ads already"; "my team cannot chase more junk leads."
- **Decision trigger:** A slow week exposes pipeline risk, or a competitor starts winning jobs in their service area.
- **Channels they trust:** Referrals, local business groups, owner podcasts, simple screenshots from ad accounts, plain-English breakdowns.

### Persona 2: The Marketing Manager — based on Competent Cog
- **Who they are:** Capable marketing operator stuck with scattered tools, unclear attribution, and owners asking for instant proof.
- **Core frustration:** They can run campaigns, but they lack a repeatable creative testing and reporting rhythm.
- **Language they use:** "I need a clean test plan," "the ad account is a mess," "we need proof before asking for more spend."
- **Objections:** "We need control"; "I cannot wait three weeks for answers"; "I need reporting the owner understands."
- **Decision trigger:** They need a credible outside partner before budget review or service-area expansion.
- **Channels they trust:** LinkedIn, agency teardown videos, detailed playbooks, ad account screenshots, benchmark emails.

### User Segments
- **Audit lead:** Requested a free Facebook ad audit or lead-cost benchmark.
- **Sales-qualified prospect:** Has a monthly ad budget, service area, sales process, and active lead follow-up.
- **Proposal open:** Received a plan but has not signed.
- **New client:** Signed and needs access, kickoff, tracking, and creative intake.
- **Active client:** Paying client receiving weekly results and creative testing direction.
- **Inactive prospect:** Went quiet after audit, call, or proposal.

## Value Prop
- **Core change:** Replace random boosted posts with a weekly creative testing system tied to booked calls.
- **Key actions:** Audit ad account, identify wasted spend, rebuild offer and lead form, test creative angles, report call outcomes.
- **Activation moment:** Prospect sees the audit score and the first three fixes ranked by revenue risk.
- **Aha moment:** Owner connects one ad angle to booked calls and can decide what to fund next.

## Business Model
- **Monetization:** Strategy call, paid audit option, monthly agency retainer, managed ad spend
- **Pricing:** Demo tiers: $2,500 setup, $3,500-$8,000 monthly retainer, ad spend separate
- **Stage:** Demo growth-stage agency

## Current Marketing
- **Channels active:** Facebook ads, referrals, LinkedIn, email nurture, owner webinars
- **Email platform:** Loops
- **Ad platforms:** Meta
- **Social:** Demo LinkedIn and YouTube
- **Content:** Ad account teardowns, benchmark reports, creative testing examples

## Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Direct, plain-spoken, operator-friendly, a little impatient with vanity metrics.
- **Banned patterns:** Fluffy agency language, vague growth promises, big claims without proof, over-polished corporate phrasing.
- **Examples:** "A lead form is not a sales process." "You do not need more ads. You need fewer guesses."

## Competitive Landscape
- **Top 3 competitors:** Local generalist agencies, freelance media buyers, CRM vendors with ad services
- **Differentiation:** Northstar links Meta creative tests to booked-call outcomes and gives owners a weekly decision rhythm.
