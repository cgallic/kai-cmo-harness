# Presenter Guide — Northstar Email System Demo

## Talk Track

### 1. Start With The Blank Page
"The client came in with a broad request: they run Facebook ads, but they do not have the customer path mapped. Kai starts by creating the brand bible and identifying the buyer, offer, segments, and conversion event."

### 2. Show The Map
"The map is the control layer. Every email has a stage, trigger, priority, segment, and job. That means the copy is not random. It is attached to behavior."

### 3. Show A Lead Example
"The first emails sell the audit and then move the prospect toward a diagnostic call. Notice how the system avoids vague agency promises and keeps asking for the next decision."

### 4. Show A Client Example
"After the sale, the email system changes from persuasion to operation. Access, kickoff, launch, weekly signal, and creative approval all keep the client moving."

### 5. Show The Loops Setup
"Kai does not stop at copy. It outputs the events, segments, fields, and flow logic needed to build the system in Loops."

### 6. Close With The System Point
"This is the Kai CMO pattern: strategy becomes map, map becomes templates, templates pass gates, and the output becomes something an operator can install."
