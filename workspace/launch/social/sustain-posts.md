### Post 4 (Apr 15) — Behind the Scenes: Quality Gates

**X/Twitter:**
The most underrated part of Kai isn't the writing. It's the rejection.

Every piece runs through 3 quality gates:
1. Four U's score (12/16 minimum)
2. Banned word check ("leverage" = instant reject)
3. SEO linter (for search content)

Most AI tools optimize for output. Kai optimizes for not shipping garbage.

**LinkedIn:**
Hot take: the most valuable part of an AI marketing tool isn't what it produces. It's what it rejects.

Kai has 3 quality gates:

1. Four U's scorer — rates every piece on Uniqueness, Usefulness, Ultra-specificity, and Urgency. Below 12/16? Rewrite.

2. Banned word detector — "leverage," "synergy," "innovative," "deep dive" trigger instant rejection. Plus AI slop phrases like "In today's rapidly evolving."

3. SEO linter — enforces 48 writing rules optimized for passage ranking and AI Overview selection.

The gates caught 40%+ of first drafts when we first turned them on. That's 40% of content that would've shipped as mediocre.
