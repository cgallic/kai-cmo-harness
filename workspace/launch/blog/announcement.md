## What these Claude Code skills do

Kai is a set of 31 marketing commands for Claude Code. Install it in 30 seconds. Type a slash command. Get finished marketing.

Not outlines. Not suggestions. Not "here are some ideas." Finished work — emails with subject lines, ad copy with character counts, blog posts with quality scores.

Here's how it works:

1. **Install Kai** — one command, 30 seconds
2. **Run a command in any project** — Kai reads your codebase, README, and any existing marketing files
3. **Get finished output** — emails, ads, blog posts, audit reports, landing page copy

Kai figures out your product on its own. You don't fill out forms. You don't answer 30 onboarding questions. It reads your code and starts working.

## What's behind the commands

Every command draws from a knowledge base we've built over 2 years:

**153 files** covering marketing frameworks, channel guides, platform-specific rules, and audience personas. Not generic advice scraped from blogs. Specific, opinionated playbooks tested on 5 live products.

**3 quality gates** run on everything Kai produces:

1. **Four U's scorer** — rates every piece on Uniqueness, Usefulness, Ultra-specificity, and Urgency. Minimum score of 12/16 for publishing.
2. **Banned word detector** — catches corporate jargon and AI slop. Trigger words get instant rejection. No "we help you unlock potential" copy survives.
3. **SEO linter** — enforces Algorithmic Authorship rules for search content. Conditions after main clauses. Verbs first. Sentences under 20 words.

**10 ad platform policy sets** — Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft, Pinterest, Snapchat, Amazon, X, plus a master FTC/GDPR/CAN-SPAM/COPPA compliance reference. Kai checks your ad copy against platform TOS before you see it.

**8 audience personas** — each with specific frustrations, language patterns, and hooks. Kai picks the right persona for your audience and writes in their language.

## Why we built it

We run 5 products: KaiCalls (AI phone agent), BuildWithKai (developer tools), Awesome Backyard Parties (event planning), VocalScribe (transcription), and ConnorGallic.com.

Five products means 5x the marketing. Five email systems. Five content calendars. Five sets of ad campaigns.

We're a small team. We can't hire 5 marketing managers. We tried agencies — they produced generic content that sounded like everyone else. We tried other AI tools — they didn't know our products, didn't enforce quality, and produced slop.

So we built Kai. First as a set of internal scripts. Then as a knowledge base. Then as Claude Code skills. Over 6 months it became the system we use every day.

## Why we're giving it away

Three reasons:

**1. The system gets better with more users.** Every bug report, feature request, and contribution makes Kai smarter. We want a community, not just customers.

**2. Marketing shouldn't require a $4K/month agency.** Solo founders and small teams deserve better than "just figure it out." Kai gives you the same frameworks and quality gates that agencies charge thousands for.

**3. It's a trojan horse (and we're fine admitting it).** We make money from KaiCalls, our AI phone agent. Kai CMO is the free tool that gets founders in the door. If you use Kai and trust the team behind it, you might try KaiCalls too. That's the business model.
