# Battlecard: KaiCalls vs CloudTalk

> Use when a prospect mentions they're "looking at CloudTalk" or "we use CloudTalk and want AI to answer calls."

## Quick Win Positioning
**"CloudTalk is a call center you have to staff and configure. KaiCalls is just the secretary — one flat price, no seats, working in 3 minutes. You don't need a call center to stop missing calls."**

## When We Win
- **Solo owner or 1–3 person service business** (HVAC, plumbing, law, medical, real estate, rental, contractors) — they don't have agents to seat.
- **They just want calls answered, leads captured, appointments booked** — not dialers, QA dashboards, and agent productivity reports.
- **They're price-sensitive** — CloudTalk's CeTe AI agent starts at €350/mo *on top of* seat licenses; KaiCalls does the same job for $69 flat.
- **They hate software/dashboards** — "just call Kai" beats learning a softphone + admin console.
- **They want month-to-month** — CloudTalk's annual lock-in and brutal cancellation policy scare burned buyers.

## When We Lose (and how to counter)
- **They genuinely have a sales/support team of 5+ agents making outbound all day.** → Don't fight it — that's a real call center need. *Counter:* "If you've got a floor of agents dialing all day, CloudTalk is built for that. KaiCalls is for catching the calls *you* can't get to. Some customers actually run both." (Honest = trust; this prospect isn't our ICP anyway.)
- **They need local numbers in 10+ countries / heavy international.** → *Counter:* concede global coverage; reframe: "If you're US-focused and the job is 'never miss a call,' the 160-country infrastructure is weight you're paying for and not using."
- **They need 100+ deep CRM integrations / Salesforce-grade workflows.** → *Counter:* "We've got CRM, SMS, email, and calendar built in so a small shop needs zero integrations. If you're running enterprise Salesforce ops, that's a different tool."

## Their Pitch (what they'll say) → our counter
- **"All-in-one platform — scale your team 10x without hiring."** → *We're not asking you to run a team. You're one owner. You don't need to scale a call center — you need the phone answered when you're on a job. That's a $69 problem, not a per-seat platform.*
- **"We have an AI Voice Agent too (CeTe)."** → *Sure — as a €350/month add-on on top of your seat licenses. With us the AI agent IS the product, starting at $69 flat. No seats to buy to unlock it.*
- **"160 countries, 99.999% uptime, conversation intelligence."** → *Built for global contact centers. If you're a local service business, that's enterprise overhead you're financing. We do one job perfectly: never miss a call.*
- **"Starts at just $19–25/user."** → *That headline doesn't include the dialer, the AI, or CRM access — real-world it's $45–55/user/month once you turn on what you actually came for. And it's per seat. Ours is flat.*

## Killer Questions to Ask the Prospect
- **"How many people on your team will actually log into it and make calls each day?"** → If the answer is "just me" or "1–2," CloudTalk's per-seat model is wrong for them.
- **"Do you want to *manage* a call center, or do you just want your phone answered and leads booked?"** → Exposes platform-overkill.
- **"When you priced their AI voice agent, did they quote you the CeTe add-on on top of the seats, or just the headline seat price?"** → Surfaces the €350/mo hidden cost.
- **"What's their cancellation policy if it's not working in month two?"** → Lands on the annual-lock / no-refund landmine.

## Landmines to Set (shift the evaluation criteria)
1. **"Per-seat vs flat"** — get them counting seats early. Every seat is a point for us.
2. **"AI agent: included vs add-on"** — make them ask CloudTalk for the *all-in* price with CeTe turned on.
3. **"Month-to-month vs annual lock-in"** — raise contract terms before they're emotionally committed; CloudTalk's billing reviews do the rest.
4. **"Time to value"** — "phone answered in 3 minutes, no app" vs configuring a call-center platform.
5. **"Do you need a dashboard at all?"** — anchor on phone-first "just call Kai"; CloudTalk can't match the no-software promise.

## One-liner for the website /compare page
> *"CloudTalk is enterprise call-center software with an AI add-on. KaiCalls is the AI secretary itself — flat $69, no seats, no contract, answering in 3 minutes. Buy the outcome, not the platform."*
