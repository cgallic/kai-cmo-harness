# Vertical Edge Brief

## Paid Ads

Best practices:
- Load platform policy before copy, targeting, creative, measurement, or API work.
- Treat API/rate docs as operational constraints, not afterthoughts.
- Keep OpenAI Ads measurement separate from creative policy until fuller official creative guidance exists.

Edge questions:
- Does the workflow have the right API tier and rate-budget for daily pulls?
- Does every CAPI/pixel event have consent state, event ID, and source-of-truth mapping?
- Is every restricted category routed to the strictest platform rule, not the easiest one?

Rejected tactics:
- Superlatives without proof.
- Hidden incentives, unapproved health/finance claims, fake urgency, or unverifiable benchmarks.
- Any API batching that tests or evades rate limits.

## Organic Social

Best practices:
- Load `social-automation-rules.md`, destination platform rules, and `social-post` quality policy.
- Use official recommendation/eligibility docs for platform-behavior claims.
- Treat social observations as patterns, not facts, unless official or measured.

Edge questions:
- Does the post need paid partnership, affiliate, AI, or synthetic-media disclosure?
- Is the workflow posting, replying, DMing, or listening through an approved API?
- Is the same asset being duplicated across accounts in a way that looks manipulative?

Rejected tactics:
- Engagement pods, fake followers, hidden brand accounts, repost farms, bot replies, and bulk DMs.

## Search and AEO

Best practices:
- Use Google Search docs for Google AI Overviews/AI Mode claims.
- Use OpenAI, Anthropic, and Perplexity crawler docs for crawler roles.
- Treat `llms.txt` as cooperative-agent support, not a Google ranking requirement.

Edge questions:
- Are search, user-triggered, and training crawlers separated in robots policy?
- Is the site blocked from AI-search surfaces because robots rules are too broad?
- Are claims about citations or traffic tied to source IDs or collector output?

Rejected tactics:
- Guaranteed AEO lift claims.
- Unsourced "ranking factor" claims.
- Grok crawler claims without official xAI documentation.

## Transcript, Video, Podcast, Webinar

Best practices:
- Load `transcript-video-research-rules.md`.
- Use timestamped pattern extraction, not transcript dumping.
- Keep direct quotes short, attributed, and approved.

Edge questions:
- Is the transcript source allowed?
- Does every quote have a timestamp or line locator?
- Does every clip candidate have usage rights and privacy status?

Rejected tactics:
- Ripping private/paywalled/login-gated content.
- Using unofficial transcript extractors without explicit rights review.
- Storing full third-party transcripts in deliverable folders.

## Content and Repurposing

Best practices:
- Build source-backed extraction maps before derivative production.
- Keep public examples as pattern evidence, not copy.
- Run channel-specific policy refs before converting one pillar into posts, clips, email, and ads.

Edge questions:
- Which claims can survive outside the source context?
- Which derivatives need fresh policy checks because the channel changes the compliance surface?
- Which quotes require guest, customer, or speaker approval?

Rejected tactics:
- Rewriting competitor creative.
- Removing caveats from source claims.
- Turning a dry-run pack into scheduled content without approval.

## Email and Newsletter

Best practices:
- Load lifecycle/cold-email rules and newsletter quality policy.
- Use CAN-SPAM and platform deliverability docs for compliance claims.
- Keep unsubscribe, sender identity, and consent basis explicit in campaign plans.

Edge questions:
- Is this transactional, lifecycle, cold, or newsletter?
- Is the audience opted in for this use?
- Do claims and examples need audit provenance?

Rejected tactics:
- Purchased lists without lawful basis.
- Fake scarcity.
- Hidden sender identity.

## CRO and Landing Pages

Best practices:
- Route paid-traffic landing pages through the matching ad policy.
- Use audit provenance for quantitative claims, speed, reviews, conversion rates, and benchmarks.
- Treat competitor pages as public patterns, not copy to reuse.

Edge questions:
- Is phone lead capture relevant for a phone-led business?
- Are testimonials, before/after, guarantees, or regulated claims present?
- Does the page collect consent cleanly?

Rejected tactics:
- Fabricated proof.
- Review counts, conversion lifts, or Core Web Vitals without collector sources.
- Hidden subscription/cancellation friction.

## Influencer, Creator, Community

Best practices:
- Load FTC disclosure, platform branded-content rules, and community rules.
- Keep creator briefs separate from paid ad policy until the asset is promoted.
- Track material connection, usage rights, exclusivity, and whitelisting rights.

Edge questions:
- Does the creator need platform-native branded-content tooling?
- Are usage rights limited by channel, duration, geography, or paid amplification?
- Is community participation transparent and rule-compliant?

Rejected tactics:
- Undisclosed endorsements.
- Astroturfing.
- Private community harvesting.

## Analytics and Attribution

Best practices:
- Load GA4, platform API, CAPI/pixel, consent, and audit provenance refs.
- Separate platform-reported metrics from CRM/revenue source of truth.
- Use missing-data ledgers instead of estimates.

Edge questions:
- Which system is source of truth for spend, revenue, leads, calls, and conversions?
- Are UTM, event ID, and consent values consistent across client/browser/server?
- Are API pull limits and freshness windows documented?

Rejected tactics:
- Invented benchmarks.
- Blended metrics without source notes.
- Client-facing numbers from screenshots without provenance.

## Launch and Product

Best practices:
- Route launch copy through channel-specific policies, especially incentives, contests, claims, and testimonials.
- Separate public observations from measured outcomes.
- Plan feedback capture and post-launch measurement before launch.

Edge questions:
- What claims need proof before launch day?
- Which channels can mutate live state and therefore need approval?
- What feedback loops need consent and privacy review?

Rejected tactics:
- Fake waitlists, fabricated social proof, undisclosed incentives, and scraping launch communities behind gates.
