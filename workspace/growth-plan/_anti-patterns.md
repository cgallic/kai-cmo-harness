# Kai Marketing OS Anti-Patterns

## Do Not Market It As A Generic AI CMO

Why it is tempting:

- "AI CMO" has search demand and sounds broad.

Why it is risky:

- The phrase is crowded and fuzzy.
- It invites comparison to dashboards, agencies, and chatbots.
- It hides Kai's strongest feature: repo-native workflow governance.

Better:

**Repo-native marketing OS for Claude Code and agentic operators.**

## Do Not Lead With Inventory Counts Alone

Why it is tempting:

- The repo is deep, and the counts are impressive.

Why it is risky:

- Counts prove effort, not value.
- Buyers need to see what they get after running it.

Better:

Lead with one before/after workflow, then use counts as support.

## Do Not Split The Brand Too Early

Why it is tempting:

- Kai, MeetKai, KaiCalls, and the harness are all real surfaces.

Why it is risky:

- New visitors need one mental model before learning the wider product set.

Better:

First explain Kai Marketing OS. Then present MeetKai as the operator layer and KaiCalls as a separate owned product only when relevant.

## Do Not Scale Paid Ads Before The Demo Converts

Why it is tempting:

- Paid tests can create quick traffic.

Why it is risky:

- The current category needs explanation. Paid spend will expose unclear messaging faster than it fixes it.

Better:

Use small paid tests only to measure message clarity after the demo and README convert organically.

## Do Not Claim AI-Search Rankings Without Measurement

Why it is tempting:

- AEO/GEO is a strong part of the product story.

Why it is risky:

- AI-search behavior is sampled, volatile, and source-dependent.

Better:

Talk about agent-readiness, source-quality, and measured visibility. Cite sources and label missing data.
