# Kai Marketing OS Budget Allocation

## Assumption

Use the early-stage budget model until revenue and retention data prove another stage.

Recommended range: **$500 to $3,000 per month plus founder time**.

## $500 Per Month

| Channel | Monthly Budget | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Demo production | $150 | Record clean clips and screenshots that prove the workflow |
| Landing page and analytics | $100 | Track install, signup, and teardown request actions |
| Community/content tools | $100 | Support publishing and scheduling |
| Small paid tests | $150 | Test message clarity, not scale |

## $1,500 Per Month

| Channel | Monthly Budget | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Demo and design polish | $300 | Better repo visuals, screenshots, and example outputs |
| Content production support | $400 | Turn real runs into posts and case studies |
| Community/account research | $250 | Find founders, agencies, educators, and repos to target |
| Paid tests | $300 | Test the flagship promise with small audiences |
| Analytics/tooling | $250 | Measure activation and source quality |

## $3,000 Per Month

| Channel | Monthly Budget | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Founder-led content system | $800 | Weekly launch essay, demos, teardown posts |
| Video and demo assets | $500 | Product-led proof for GitHub, site, X, LinkedIn |
| Community and partnership work | $400 | Newsletter, educator, and boilerplate outreach |
| Paid tests | $600 | Message and offer testing after organic proof appears |
| Analytics and activation tracking | $300 | Understand install-to-output conversion |
| Contract help | $400 | Polish docs, landing page, or examples |

## Budget Rule

Do not scale paid media until these are true:

- GitHub README conversion is clear enough for a cold visitor
- A demo shows a useful output in under 3 minutes
- At least 10 real users or prospects have run, watched, or requested a Kai workflow
- The team knows which promise drives activation: plan, landing page, ads, email, SEO, or audit
