# Creator & Personal Brand Audit Checklist

> **Use when:** Auditing a business where the individual IS the brand and monetizes through audience attention. Best fit for content creators, solopreneurs, coaches, course creators, newsletter operators, YouTubers, podcasters, authors, speakers, and influencers who sell digital products, services, sponsorships, or community access.

---

## Owned Audience (Email / Newsletter)

> **Why it matters:** Social platforms can throttle reach, change algorithms, or disappear. An email list is the only audience asset you fully own. For creators, it is the single most reliable path to revenue.

- [ ] Email list exists and is actively growing
- [ ] Newsletter cadence is consistent (weekly minimum)
- [ ] At least one lead magnet exists for list building (checklist, template, mini-course, swipe file, etc.)
- [ ] Welcome sequence is live for new subscribers (3-7 emails minimum)
- [ ] List is segmented: prospects vs customers vs sponsors/partners
- [ ] Email platform is appropriate for stage (ConvertKit, Beehiiv, Substack, Ghost, etc.)
- [ ] Open rate is tracked and benchmarked (target 40%+ for creator lists)
- [ ] Click-through rate is tracked on every send
- [ ] Sponsorship rate card exists if monetizing the newsletter
- [ ] Deliverability hygiene is maintained: list cleaned quarterly, domain authenticated (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
- [ ] Unsubscribe and re-engagement flows exist for inactive subscribers
- [ ] Newsletter archive is publicly accessible for social proof and SEO

---

## Primary Content Platform

> **Why it matters:** Creators who spread thin across every platform grow nowhere. Dominance on one platform builds compounding returns before expanding to others.

- [ ] One primary platform is identified and prioritized (YouTube, podcast, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, blog)
- [ ] Posting cadence is consistent (minimum 3x/week for social, 1x/week for long-form)
- [ ] Content pillars are defined (3-5 core topics the audience expects)
- [ ] Content repurposing pipeline exists (long-form -> clips -> social -> newsletter)
- [ ] Analytics are tracked: subscriber/follower growth rate, engagement rate, views/listens per post
- [ ] SEO optimization is applied on discoverable platforms (YouTube titles/descriptions, podcast show notes, blog on-page SEO)
- [ ] Collaboration strategy is active (guest appearances, features, shoutouts, duets)
- [ ] Content backlog or batching system prevents missed posts
- [ ] Top-performing content is identified and formats are repeated
- [ ] Platform-specific best practices are followed (hooks in first 3 seconds for video, thread structure for X, etc.)

---

## Monetization Engine

> **Why it matters:** Audience without monetization is a hobby. A clear revenue model with multiple price points turns attention into sustainable income.

- [ ] Revenue model is clearly defined (digital products, courses, coaching, community, sponsorships, affiliate, SaaS, services)
- [ ] Product ladder exists: free -> low-ticket ($10-50) -> mid-ticket ($100-500) -> high-ticket ($500+)
- [ ] Sales page exists for the primary paid offer
- [ ] Checkout and payment system is set up and tested (Gumroad, Teachable, Kajabi, Stripe, Podia, etc.)
- [ ] Upsell and cross-sell paths are defined (buyers of X are offered Y)
- [ ] Pricing strategy is intentional: anchor pricing, bundles, or cohort-based vs evergreen considered
- [ ] Revenue is diversified across at least 2 income streams (not 100% dependent on sponsorships or one product)
- [ ] Sponsorship or affiliate income is tracked separately from product revenue
- [ ] Revenue per subscriber is calculated and tracked monthly

---

## Personal Brand Positioning

> **Why it matters:** Creators who cannot articulate what they are known for get lost in the noise. Clear positioning turns a content feed into a brand people seek out and recommend.

- [ ] Clear niche and positioning statement exists ("I help [who] do [what] through [how]")
- [ ] Unique angle, methodology, or framework differentiates from peers
- [ ] Visual brand is consistent across all platforms (professional headshot, colors, fonts, thumbnail style)
- [ ] Bio and about page tell a compelling origin story with credibility markers
- [ ] Brand voice is documented and consistent across platforms
- [ ] "Known for" factor is identifiable: what do people associate with this person?
- [ ] Positioning is validated by audience feedback (DMs, comments, testimonials reference the core message)
- [ ] Brand is not dependent on a single platform's trends or format (the person is the brand, not the medium)

---

## Community & Engagement

> **Why it matters:** Audience is passive. Community is active. Creators who build community get higher retention, better product feedback, stronger word-of-mouth, and more resilient revenue.

- [ ] Community exists in a dedicated space (Discord, Circle, Skool, Facebook Group, membership site, Slack)
- [ ] Community engagement is active and two-way (not just broadcast/announcements)
- [ ] DM strategy exists: DMs are used for relationship building, not ignored
- [ ] Comment engagement on primary platform is consistent (responding to comments within 24 hours)
- [ ] Audience feedback loop is active: polls, surveys, AMA sessions, or "reply to this email" prompts
- [ ] Super-fans are identified and cultivated (early access, shoutouts, ambassador programs)
- [ ] Community has clear value proposition for members beyond "access to the creator"
- [ ] Moderation and community guidelines are established

---

## Website & Hub

> **Why it matters:** Social profiles are rented storefronts. A central website is the owned home base that ties everything together, captures emails, and converts visitors who arrive from any channel.

- [ ] Central website or landing page exists
- [ ] Email capture is prominent on every page (popup, inline form, sticky bar, or embedded)
- [ ] Social proof is visible: "as seen in" logos, testimonials, student/client results, subscriber count
- [ ] Clear "start here" path exists for new visitors (start here page, quiz, or guided flow)
- [ ] SEO-optimized blog exists if blog is a content channel
- [ ] Link-in-bio page is optimized and current (Linktree, Beacons, Stan Store, or custom page)
- [ ] Speaking, consulting, or booking page exists if applicable
- [ ] Media kit or press page exists for sponsorship and press inquiries
- [ ] Website loads in under 3 seconds on mobile
- [ ] Site uses HTTPS
- [ ] Analytics are installed (Google Analytics, Fathom, Plausible, or PostHog)

---

## Digital Product & Course Marketing

> **Why it matters:** Most creators launch once and move on. The highest-revenue creators build evergreen systems that sell while they sleep and launch sequences that compound with each release.

- [ ] Launch sequence exists for new products (pre-launch tease, launch week, post-launch follow-up)
- [ ] Evergreen funnel exists for at least one product (webinar, email sequence, or sales page running continuously)
- [ ] Student or customer testimonials and results are collected and displayed
- [ ] Free workshop, webinar, or challenge serves as top-of-funnel for paid products
- [ ] Cart abandonment follow-up sequence is live
- [ ] Product updates and improvements are communicated to past buyers
- [ ] Affiliate or referral program exists for courses and products
- [ ] Launch debrief is completed after every launch (what worked, what to improve, revenue breakdown)

---

## Collaboration & Network

> **Why it matters:** Creators who operate in isolation grow linearly. Collaborations, guest appearances, and partnerships unlock exponential audience sharing and credibility transfer.

- [ ] Guest appearances on other podcasts, YouTube channels, or newsletters happen regularly
- [ ] Collaborative content with peers is published (joint lives, co-authored pieces, crossover episodes)
- [ ] Affiliate partnerships exist with complementary products or creators
- [ ] Mastermind or peer group membership is active
- [ ] Event speaking is pursued (virtual summits, conferences, workshops, in-person events)
- [ ] Cross-promotion with complementary creators happens on a recurring basis
- [ ] Media and press outreach is executed for major launches or milestones
- [ ] Outreach tracking system exists (who to contact, when, follow-up status)

---

## Analytics & Growth

> **Why it matters:** Creators who do not measure cannot improve. Tracking the right numbers exposes what is actually driving revenue versus what just feels productive.

- [ ] Growth rate is tracked across all platforms monthly (follower/subscriber count, not just vanity metrics)
- [ ] Revenue per subscriber is calculated (total revenue / total email subscribers)
- [ ] Content performance is analyzed regularly: which topics, formats, and hooks drive engagement
- [ ] Funnel conversion rates are tracked: visitor -> subscriber -> customer
- [ ] Attribution tracking exists: which platform drives the most revenue
- [ ] A/B testing is applied to landing pages, email subject lines, and offers
- [ ] Monthly or quarterly growth review is conducted with documented takeaways
- [ ] Benchmarks are set for each platform and progress is measured against them

## Scoring

Rate each section 1-5:

| Section | Score (1-5) | Weight | Notes |
|---------|:-----------:|:------:|-------|
| Owned Audience (Email / Newsletter) | | 5x | Your only fully owned channel |
| Primary Content Platform | | 5x | Compounding growth engine |
| Monetization Engine | | 4x | Turns attention into income |
| Personal Brand Positioning | | 4x | Differentiation and recall |
| Community & Engagement | | 3x | Retention and word-of-mouth |
| Website & Hub | | 3x | Owned home base and conversion |
| Digital Product & Course Marketing | | 2x | Scalable revenue systems |
| Collaboration & Network | | 2x | Exponential audience growth |
| Analytics & Growth | | 2x | Informed decision-making |

**Weighted score:** `/150` -> normalize to `/100` for the audit report.

**How to calculate:** Multiply each section score by its weight. Sum all weighted scores. Divide by 150. Multiply by 100.

**Grading:**
- A: 90+
- B: 75-89
- C: 60-74
- D: 40-59
- F: <40

## Exclusions

This checklist does **not** cover:

- Physical product businesses or e-commerce operations
- Agency or team-based businesses where the founder is not the brand
- Platform-specific ad policy compliance (see `harness/references/` for ad rules)
- Technical SEO audits (see `knowledge/checklists/technical-seo-audit-sop.md`)
- Legal, tax, or business entity formation
- Multi-brand or media company operations
