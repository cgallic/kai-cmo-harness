# Google AI Mode: Agentic Features

> **Use when:** Planning AEO/SEO strategy for sites whose buyers research, plan, or book via Google; advising hospitality, ticketing, beauty/wellness, retail, or travel clients; sizing the threat or opportunity of agentic search for any local-services business.

Source packet: `knowledge/research/google-agentic-search/`.

---

## What changed

In November 2025 Google began moving Search from a "ten blue links + AI Overview" surface into an **agentic plan-and-act surface**. Three concrete capabilities are now live or near-live in the U.S.:

1. **Canvas in AI Mode** — a persistent side-panel itinerary builder. AI Mode pulls real-time Search data for flights and hotels, Google Maps photos/reviews, and information from sites across the web into a single revisable plan. The user refines via follow-up dialogue.
2. **Flight Deals** — a conversational airfare search inside Google Flights. Expanded to 200+ countries and 60+ languages.
3. **Agentic booking** — AI Mode searches reservation platforms in real time and lands the user on a curated list. Restaurant booking shipped to all U.S. users (no Labs opt-in). Event tickets and beauty/wellness appointments are in U.S. Labs. Flights and hotels are next.

Reservation/booking partners announced: OpenTable, Resy, Tock, Ticketmaster, StubHub, SeatGeek, Vivid Seats, Booksy, Fresha, Vagaro.

Hotel partners announced: Booking.com, Choice Hotels International, Expedia, IHG Hotels & Resorts, Marriott International, Wyndham Hotels & Resorts.

A separate "Let Google call" feature lets Search users ask Google to call local businesses for retail intent (toys, health and beauty, electronics in the U.S.). See `knowledge/research/google-agentic-search/agentic-calling.md`.

## What this means for AEO and content strategy

### The destination shifts from your page to AI Mode's plan

For categories where Google now ships an agentic plan (travel, restaurants, events, beauty/wellness), the user's session can complete inside AI Mode. The page that "wins" is less the destination and more the **structured data and partner integration that feeds the plan**. A great hotel landing page that isn't legible to Google's plan-builder loses to a mediocre listing inside an integrated partner.

Implications:

- Treat schema.org and structured availability data as competitive infrastructure, not hygiene.
- Optimize partner-channel presence in OpenTable / Resy / Tock / Booksy / Fresha / Vagaro / ticketing platforms if the business is in those verticals — they are the API surface AI Mode reads.
- Hotels with direct integrations (Booking.com, Marriott, IHG, Wyndham, Choice, Expedia) will be surfaced first in the future AI Mode flight/hotel booking flow. Smaller properties without partner presence risk invisibility.

### Conversational intent replaces keyword intent

Canvas works because users describe trips conversationally ("closer to brunch but a bit further from hiking"). Content that already reads as answers to natural-language questions wins. Content optimized for short-tail keywords loses.

- Write to answer trade-off questions, not single-attribute queries.
- Maintain entity-rich pages with named amenities, times, distances, and price ranges so AI Mode can lift facts cleanly.
- Add comparison artifacts (tables, decision rules) that AI Mode can summarize without inventing.

### Local-services SERP is increasingly agentic

For retail-style queries with "near me," Google now offers to **call local businesses on the user's behalf**. This is a parallel agentic path to AI Mode's reservation booking. Combined, the local SERP for a service business looks like:

- Map pack (existing)
- AI Overview / AI Mode (entity- and plan-led)
- "Let Google call" CTA (for retail intent)
- Partner reservation block (for restaurant / appointment intent)
- Organic blue links (compressed below the fold)

Local-services SEO strategy must now also include phone-readiness — see the KaiCalls competitive note in `knowledge/research/google-agentic-search/kaicalls-competitive-implications.md`.

## Operator rules

- **For hospitality clients:** confirm presence on at least one announced reservation/hotel partner. Audit menu/availability data freshness. Treat AI Mode listings as a primary distribution channel, not an afterthought.
- **For ticketing/event clients:** monitor inventory visibility across StubHub, Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, Vivid Seats — AI Mode pulls these in parallel.
- **For beauty/wellness clients:** Booksy, Fresha, Vagaro are now AI Mode's plumbing. Profile completeness on these platforms outranks website SEO for many intent paths.
- **For retail clients:** prepare for inbound AI calls (text/email summaries imply a wider net of called businesses). Phone-readiness, after-hours coverage, and consistent product/price availability across channels become the win conditions.
- **For travel content sites:** the value of "best of" listicles drops sharply once Canvas can assemble personalized comparison plans. Pivot to opinion, on-the-ground reporting, and trade-off coverage AI Mode can't fabricate.
- **For all clients:** track when the analogous agentic flow lands in your client's vertical. Restaurants, events, and appointments are live. Flights and hotels are explicitly next. Legal services, home services, financial services, and B2B are unannounced but plausible.

## What we still don't know

- Exact AI Mode ranking weights for partner listings vs. organic.
- Whether AI Mode honors site-level signals that worked for AI Overviews.
- Disclosure language Google uses when "Let Google call" places a call to a business.
- Whether businesses can opt out of receiving agentic calls.
- Call-volume implications for businesses in retail verticals.

Treat all five as live AEO research questions.
