# Multi-Channel Campaign Orchestration Playbook

> **Use when**: Planning and executing multi-channel marketing campaigns, coordinating launch sequences, building campaign timelines, assigning assets per channel, measuring campaign performance.

---

## Campaign Types

| Type | Duration | Channels | Primary Goal | Budget Range |
|------|----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Product Launch** | 8 weeks pre + 4 weeks post | All channels | Awareness → Adoption | High |
| **Feature Release** | 2 weeks pre + 2 weeks post | Email, social, blog, in-app | Adoption → Engagement | Low-medium |
| **Promotional** | 1-2 weeks | Email, paid, social, landing page | Revenue / Conversion | Medium |
| **Webinar/Event** | 4 weeks pre + 2 weeks post | Email, social, paid, landing page | Pipeline / Lead Gen | Medium |
| **Seasonal** | 2-4 weeks | Email, social, paid | Revenue / Engagement | Medium |
| **Awareness/Brand** | Ongoing (8-12 week flights) | Content, social, PR, paid | SOV / Brand Lift | High |
| **Re-engagement** | 2-4 weeks | Email, retargeting, push | Win-back / Activation | Low |

## Master Timeline Template

### Product Launch (8-week pre + 4-week post)

| Week | Phase | Key Activities |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **W-8** | Strategy | Define goals, KPIs, audience segments, budget allocation. Lock messaging framework. |
| **W-7** | Planning | Build asset list per channel. Assign owners. Set up project management board. |
| **W-6** | Content Creation | Write landing page copy, email sequences, blog posts, ad copy, social content. |
| **W-5** | Creative Production | Design landing page, ad creatives, social graphics, video assets. |
| **W-4** | Review & QA | Internal review of all assets. Legal/compliance review. A/B test variants ready. |
| **W-3** | Pre-launch Tease | Teaser emails to VIP list. Social hints. Influencer/partner seeding. PR embargo pitches. |
| **W-2** | Technical Setup | Landing page live (unlisted). Tracking pixels installed. UTMs configured. Email sequences loaded. Ad campaigns built (paused). |
| **W-1** | Final Prep | Dry run of launch sequence. Team briefing. Sales enablement materials distributed. |
| **Launch Day** | Go Live | Landing page public. Email blast. Social posts. Ads activated. PR embargo lifts. Blog post published. |
| **W+1** | Amplify | Retargeting activated. Social engagement (reply to every comment). Partner amplification. |
| **W+2** | Optimize | Review first-week data. Pause underperforming ads. Double down on winning channels. |
| **W+3** | Sustain | Publish supporting content (case studies, tutorials, comparisons). Second email wave. |
| **W+4** | Retrospective | Campaign retrospective meeting. Document learnings. Final performance report. |

### Webinar/Event Campaign (4-week pre + 2-week post)

| Week | Phase | Key Activities |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **W-4** | Setup | Create registration landing page. Build email sequence (invite + 3 reminders). Design social graphics. |
| **W-3** | Announce | Email invite to full list. Social announcement. LinkedIn event creation. Partner cross-promotion. |
| **W-2** | Drive Registrations | Paid ads targeting lookalike audience. Retargeting site visitors. Social proof posts ("500 registered!"). |
| **W-1** | Final Push | Scarcity email ("Last chance to register"). Reminder sequence starts. Speaker prep. Dry run. |
| **Event Day** | Execute | Go live. Chat moderation. Q&A capture. Social live-tweets. Recording started. |
| **W+1** | Follow Up | Recording sent to registrants + no-shows. Attendee survey. Sales follow-up on hot leads. |
| **W+2** | Repurpose | Blog post recap. Social clips (5-10 short clips). Slides on SlideShare. Transcript for SEO. |

## Channel Coordination Matrix

For each campaign, define which channels are active and their role.

| Channel | Role | Asset Needed | Owner | Timing |
|---------|------|-------------|-------|--------|
| **Landing page** | Convert — primary destination | Page copy, design, form | Marketing | Live by W-2 |
| **Email (blast)** | Drive — send traffic to LP | 1-3 broadcast emails | Email team | Launch day + W+1 |
| **Email (sequence)** | Nurture — post-registration/sign-up | 5-7 automated emails | Email team | Active from trigger event |
| **Blog** | Educate + SEO — supporting content | 1-3 blog posts | Content team | Launch day + W+1, W+3 |
| **Social (organic)** | Amplify — reach existing audience | 10-15 posts across platforms | Social team | W-3 through W+4 |
| **Social (paid)** | Acquire — reach new audience | Ad creatives (3+ variants) | Paid team | Launch day through W+3 |
| **Search ads** | Capture — intercept intent | Ad copy + landing page | Paid team | Launch day ongoing |
| **Retargeting** | Recover — bring back non-converters | Display/social retargeting ads | Paid team | W+1 through W+4 |
| **PR** | Authority — media coverage | Press release, media kit | PR/comms | Launch day (embargo lift) |
| **Partner** | Extend — leverage partner audiences | Co-branded assets, partner emails | Partnerships | W-1 through W+2 |
| **Sales enablement** | Close — arm sales team | One-pager, battlecard, demo script | Product marketing | W-1 (pre-launch) |

## Asset Checklist Per Channel

### Landing Page

```
[ ] Headline (< 70 characters)
[ ] Subheadline (< 120 characters)
[ ] Hero image or video
[ ] 3-5 benefit bullets
[ ] Social proof section (logos, testimonials, stats)
[ ] Primary CTA button (above fold + bottom of page)
[ ] FAQ section (5-7 questions)
[ ] Form (lead gen) or purchase flow (sales)
[ ] Mobile-responsive design tested
[ ] Page speed < 3 seconds
[ ] Tracking pixel installed (Meta, Google, LinkedIn)
[ ] UTM parameter landing detection working
[ ] Thank you / confirmation page
[ ] Open Graph meta tags (title, description, image)
```

### Email Sequence (5-email standard)

```
[ ] Email 1: Announcement / Invitation — sent Day 0
[ ] Email 2: Value / Education — sent Day 2
[ ] Email 3: Social Proof — sent Day 4
[ ] Email 4: Objection Handling — sent Day 6
[ ] Email 5: Urgency / Last Chance — sent Day 7-10
[ ] Subject lines: 3 variants per email for A/B testing
[ ] Preview text optimized (40-90 characters)
[ ] UTM parameters on all links
[ ] Unsubscribe link present
[ ] Mobile rendering tested
[ ] Personalization tokens working (first name, company)
[ ] Segmentation rules applied (exclude existing customers, etc.)
```

### Social Posts (per platform)

```
[ ] 3-5 pre-launch teaser posts
[ ] 1 launch announcement post
[ ] 3-5 supporting value posts (tips, stats, behind-the-scenes)
[ ] 1-2 social proof posts (testimonials, results)
[ ] 1 urgency/scarcity post
[ ] Platform-specific formatting applied
[ ] Hashtags per platform guidelines (3-5 LinkedIn, 1-2 X, 3-5 Instagram)
[ ] Media assets (images, carousels, short video clips)
[ ] UTM parameters on all links
[ ] Publishing schedule loaded into scheduler
```

### Paid Ads

```
[ ] 3+ creative variants per platform (A/B/C testing)
[ ] Headline variants (5+ for RSA, 3+ for Meta)
[ ] Description variants
[ ] Landing page URL with UTMs
[ ] Audience targeting defined (prospecting + retargeting)
[ ] Budget allocated per channel and campaign type
[ ] Conversion tracking verified
[ ] Negative keywords set (Google Ads)
[ ] Frequency caps set (Display/Social)
[ ] Ad scheduling configured
```

### PR / Press Release

```
[ ] Press release written (inverted pyramid)
[ ] Media kit (logos, product screenshots, founder headshot)
[ ] Targeted journalist list (20-50 contacts)
[ ] Embargo date set and communicated
[ ] Pitch email drafted (personalized per journalist)
[ ] Quotes from spokesperson approved
[ ] Boilerplate company description current
[ ] Distribution wire selected (if using)
```

## UTM Parameter Strategy

Consistent UTM tagging is non-negotiable for campaign attribution.

### Standard UTM structure

```
?utm_source=[platform]
&utm_medium=[channel_type]
&utm_campaign=[campaign_name]
&utm_content=[asset_variant]
&utm_term=[keyword_or_audience]  (optional, for paid only)
```

### UTM conventions

| Parameter | Convention | Examples |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| `utm_source` | Platform name (lowercase) | `google`, `facebook`, `linkedin`, `email`, `twitter` |
| `utm_medium` | Channel type | `cpc`, `organic_social`, `email`, `display`, `referral`, `partner` |
| `utm_campaign` | Campaign name (kebab-case) | `product-launch-2026-q1`, `webinar-seo-masterclass`, `summer-sale` |
| `utm_content` | Asset identifier | `email-1-cta`, `carousel-variant-b`, `hero-banner`, `sidebar-ad` |
| `utm_term` | Keyword or audience | `brand-keywords`, `lookalike-1pct`, `retargeting-30d` |

### Rules

1. **Always lowercase** — Prevents duplicate entries in analytics.
2. **Kebab-case** — Use hyphens, not underscores or spaces.
3. **Be specific** — `email-3-urgency-cta` is better than `email`.
4. **Document in a spreadsheet** — Maintain a UTM log for the campaign. Every URL with parameters gets logged.
5. **Use a UTM builder** — Google's Campaign URL Builder or a shared spreadsheet template to prevent typos.
6. **Test before launch** — Click every UTM link and verify it appears correctly in Google Analytics real-time view.

## Budget Allocation Model

### Default allocation by campaign type

| Channel | Launch | Webinar | Promotional | Awareness |
|---------|--------|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **Paid search** | 20% | 15% | 30% | 10% |
| **Paid social** | 25% | 25% | 25% | 30% |
| **Display/retargeting** | 15% | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| **Content/SEO** | 15% | 10% | 5% | 25% |
| **Email** | 5% | 15% | 15% | 5% |
| **PR** | 10% | 5% | 0% | 10% |
| **Creative production** | 10% | 10% | 10% | 0% |
| **Reserve (testing)** | 0% | 10% | 0% | 0% |

### Budget reallocation rules

1. **Week 1**: Distribute budget as planned. Do not optimize yet.
2. **Week 2**: Review CPA/ROAS by channel. Shift 10-20% from worst performer to best performer.
3. **Week 3+**: Aggressive reallocation. Pause channels with CPA > 2x target. Double budget on channels below target CPA.
4. **Reserve budget**: Hold 10% of total budget for mid-campaign testing (new audiences, new creatives, new channels).

## A/B Testing Framework

### What to test per channel

| Channel | Test Variables | Metric | Min Duration |
|---------|---------------|--------|-------------|
| **Landing page** | Headline, CTA copy, hero image, form length, social proof placement | Conversion rate | 2 weeks or 200 conversions |
| **Email** | Subject line, send time, CTA button text, personalization | Open rate, click rate | 1 send per variant (1,000+ recipients) |
| **Paid ads** | Creative, headline, audience, bid strategy | CTR, CPA, ROAS | 7 days or 50 conversions per variant |
| **Social** | Hook, format (image vs video vs carousel), posting time | Engagement rate, reach | 3-5 posts per variant |

### Testing rules

1. **One variable at a time** — Multivariate testing requires massive sample sizes. Stick to A/B (single variable).
2. **Statistical significance** — Don't call a winner until you have 95% confidence. Use an A/B test calculator.
3. **Document everything** — Every test hypothesis, variant, result, and learning gets logged.
4. **Iterate, don't abandon** — A losing test is still a learning. Document why and test the next hypothesis.
