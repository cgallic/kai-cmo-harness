## Facet: Key People & Org Structure -- LinkedIn AI Teams

*Research conducted across LinkedIn Engineering Blog, arXiv publications, conference speaker lists, news articles, and press releases. Last updated: June 2025.*

---

### Key Findings

- LinkedIn appointed **Deepak Agarwal** as **Chief AI Officer** in January 2025, marking his second tenure at the company after serving as VP of AI from 2012-2020 [^9^][^10^]. He leads the Core AI teams and company-wide AI strategy.
- **Ya Xu** served as VP of Engineering and Head of Data & AI, leading ~1,000 data scientists and AI engineers, but reportedly left for Google DeepMind in September 2024 [^159^][^150^].
- LinkedIn has a dedicated **Foundation AI Technologies (FAIT)** team led by **Hamed Firooz** that built **360Brew**, a 150B-parameter foundation model for personalized ranking and recommendation [^48^][^76^].
- The **Feed Sequential Recommender (Feed SR)** team includes authors **Lars Hertel**, **Gaurav Srivastava**, and others, who published at arXiv in February 2026 [^51^].
- **Karthik Ramgopal** serves as Distinguished Engineer and Uber Tech Lead for Product Engineering, overseeing ~5,000 engineers across all member-facing products, with recent focus on Generative AI [^96^][^97^].
- The **Anti-Abuse AI Team** includes **James Verbus** (Staff ML Engineer) working on deep learning models for abuse detection [^82^].
- **Qingquan Song**, a co-author on multiple LinkedIn AI papers (360Brew, LiNR), left LinkedIn for OpenAI in 2025 [^143^].
- **Tim Jurka** rose from entry-level engineer to **VP of Engineering**, leading cross-functional AI, product engineering, infrastructure, and data platform teams [^75^][^53^].

### Trends & Signals

- **Leadership Transition (Jan 2025)**: Deepak Agarwal returned as Chief AI Officer, bringing Pinterest experience in scaling AI orgs from 200 to 1,000 engineers. Signals LinkedIn's intent to significantly scale AI operations [^9^][^10^][^127^].
- **Foundation Model Strategy**: LinkedIn's FAIT team built 360Brew, a 150B-parameter decoder-only model on LLaMA 3 architecture, trained exclusively on LinkedIn's proprietary data. It replaced five separate retrieval pipelines and handles 30+ predictive tasks [^48^][^46^].
- **Feed Architecture Revolution (2024-2026)**: LinkedIn replaced legacy feature-engineering-based feed ranking with unified LLM-based retrieval and Generative Recommender (GR) sequential models. March 2026 public announcement by Hristo Danchev [^53^][^17^].
- **Talent Mobility**: Qingquan Song (co-author on 360Brew, LiNR) left for OpenAI in 2025 [^143^]. Ya Xu reportedly left for Google DeepMind in September 2024 [^159^]. This indicates competitive pressure from top AI labs.
- **Agentic AI Deployment**: LinkedIn launched Hiring Assistant (October 2024), its first large-scale AI agent using supervisor/sub-agent architecture with human-in-the-loop controls. Built on LinkedIn's agent platform with gRPC service schemas and messaging-based orchestration [^98^][^103^].
- **Trust & Safety Investment**: Daniel Olmedilla (Senior Engineering Director, 100+ publications) leads Responsible AI efforts. James Verbus leads deep learning anti-abuse models. Trust Data Science team hiring Senior Staff Data Scientists [^129^][^82^][^101^].

### Controversies & Conflicting Claims

- **Ya Xu Departure**: Multiple anonymous Blind posts (September 2024) claim Ya Xu was "forced out" of LinkedIn and her "entire org got ripped away and handed to someone else" before joining Google DeepMind. Other comments defend her, saying "she set AI progress forward by 10 years" [^159^]. LinkedIn never publicly confirmed her departure in these terms. These claims are unverified but suggest organizational tensions.
- **360Brew Impact on Reach**: AuthoredUp study found 98% of users experienced reach decline (median -47%) after 360Brew deployment. LinkedIn has stated this is intentional -- the algorithm prioritizes relevance over broad distribution [^46^][^50^].
- **AI Content Detection**: LinkedIn's system now flags AI-generated content patterns, but the company does not penalize AI-assisted content that is reviewed and edited. An Originality.ai study found likely AI-generated posts received 45% fewer interactions [^16^].

### Recommended Deep-Dive Areas

1. **Deepak Agarwal's AI Strategy**: His second tenure as CAIO, plans to scale the AI org, and his Pinterest-honed approach to unifying AI Foundations, Consumer Engineering, and Trust & Safety. High strategic relevance given his mandate to make LinkedIn "AI-first" [^127^].

2. **FAIT Team & 360Brew Evolution**: Hamed Firooz's 50-person team building foundation models. The 150B-parameter 360Brew V1.0 and its deployment across 30+ tasks. Follow-up work on efficient LLMs and model compression [^48^][^76^].

3. **Feed Ranking Architecture**: The transition from legacy ranking to LLM-based retrieval (Causal LLM on LLaMA-3) plus Generative Recommender sequential models. Lars Hertel and Gaurav Srivastava's Feed SR work. Hristo Danchev's infrastructure work [^53^][^51^][^17^].

4. **Trust & Safety AI**: Daniel Olmedilla's Responsible AI framework, James Verbus's deep learning anti-abuse models, and the Trust Data Science team's metrics work. Critical for understanding LinkedIn's approach to fake accounts, scraping, and AI-generated content detection [^129^][^82^][^101^].

5. **Agentic AI Platform**: Karthik Ramgopal's leadership on the Hiring Assistant and LinkedIn's broader agent platform. The plan-and-execute architecture, messaging-based orchestration, and human-in-the-loop design patterns [^98^][^96^].

6. **Talent Flow**: Tracking departures (Ya Xu to DeepMind, Qingquan Song to OpenAI) and new hires. Understanding competitive dynamics with other AI labs and how this affects LinkedIn's AI roadmap [^143^][^159^].
