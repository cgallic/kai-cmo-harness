# Sales, Pricing, And Packaging

> **Use when:** Turning market signals, sales calls, CRM notes, competitor observations, usage data, or pricing pages into offer, package, pricing, and follow-up recommendations.

---

## Core Thesis

Pricing work changes buyer expectations and revenue mechanics. Treat it as a high-risk recommendation layer, not casual copywriting.

Kai can synthesize signals, model options, and prepare approval-ready packages. Kai must not change live prices, send offers, update CRM stages, or enroll contacts without approval.

---

## Source Inputs

| Source | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Sales-call transcripts | Objections, buying triggers, urgency, willingness-to-pay signals |
| CRM exports | Stage movement, close reasons, deal size, segment fit |
| Pricing-page data | Current package structure and friction points |
| Support or success notes | Retention risk, expansion signals, feature value |
| Competitor pages | Public packaging comparison with retrieval date |
| Analytics | Pricing-page behavior, form starts, checkout, calls, demo requests |
| Finance data | Margin, CAC, payback, capacity, service cost |

Quantitative claims require source refs. Missing CRM, finance, or conversion data belongs in `_data-gaps.md`.

---

## Analysis Layers

| Layer | Question |
|-------|----------|
| ICP fit | Which buyer segment is this package for? |
| Pain urgency | What cost does the package remove? |
| Value metric | What should scale price: seats, usage, locations, revenue, calls, projects, or support level? |
| Offer shape | Productized service, retainer, subscription, usage, performance, hybrid, or enterprise |
| Proof | What evidence supports value and risk reduction? |
| Objection handling | Which objections must packaging answer before the call? |
| Operational cost | Can delivery support the promise? |
| Compliance | Are claims, guarantees, and comparisons substantiated? |

---

## Packaging Options

Kai should usually produce options, not one forced answer:

- Conservative: lowest disruption, keeps current buyer expectations.
- Focused: narrows package around the strongest ICP and highest-value problem.
- Expansion: adds tiering, usage, add-ons, or enterprise path.

Each option needs tradeoffs, not only upside.

---

## Workflow

1. Declare mode and source inventory.
2. Sanitize CRM exports, transcripts, and screenshots.
3. Extract objections, buying triggers, deal friction, package confusion, and proof gaps.
4. Compare current package against ICP, margin, delivery capacity, and conversion friction.
5. Draft package options with assumptions and confidence.
6. Produce a dry-run pricing memo.
7. Request approval before changing pages, sending offers, updating CRM, launching tests, or briefing sales.
8. Route approved changes into the experimentation ledger.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent revenue, conversion, win-rate, churn, or margin data.
- Do not claim a price is "optimal" without experiment evidence.
- Do not use fake expert authority or simulated buyer panels as proof.
- Do not make live pricing, CRM, or sales-sequence changes without approval.
- Do not recommend phone-led automation unless phone demand and fit signals are present.
