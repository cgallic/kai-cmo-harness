# Growth Hacker First-Hire Checklist

> **Use when:** Validating a `/kai-growth-hacker` package before the founder acts on channel recommendations or assigns specialist execution.

## Context

- [ ] `MARKETING.md` or a temporary product brief is loaded.
- [ ] Business type is declared: B2B, B2C, marketplace, local service, ecommerce, creator, or mixed.
- [ ] Stage is declared: pre-launch, first revenue, early growth, growth, or scale.
- [ ] Primary conversion is declared: waitlist, trial, demo, call, purchase, subscription, event registration, or partner lead.
- [ ] Existing channels, failed channels, and current proof assets are listed.
- [ ] Budget, operator time, and production capacity are listed.
- [ ] Regulated or sensitive categories are declared.

## Channel Coverage

- [ ] B2B channel map covers LinkedIn, events, AI outbound, AEO, blogs, long-form writing, YouTube, webinars, X, influencers, partnerships, lifecycle, podcast, community, PR, paid media, and retargeting.
- [ ] B2C channel map covers AI UGC, organic TikTok, paid social, events, influencer, sponsorships, email/SMS, referral, affiliate, community, Instagram/Reels, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, Snapchat, and relevant commerce surfaces.
- [ ] Each channel has fit, inputs, execution loop, Kai skill routing, gates, metrics, kill rule, and next test.
- [ ] Blocked channels explain the missing data, compliance issue, budget issue, offer issue, or tracking issue.

## Prioritization

- [ ] Every channel is scored on audience density, message fit, speed to signal, cost to test, compounding value, compliance risk, and operator advantage.
- [ ] One primary channel is selected.
- [ ] One secondary channel is selected.
- [ ] One exploratory channel is selected.
- [ ] Kill rules are written before any test starts.
- [ ] Graduation rules are written before any test starts.

## Fan-Out Plan

- [ ] Growth Lead owns the final scorecard and 90-day sprint.
- [ ] Evidence Scout owns source packets and data gaps.
- [ ] B2B Channel Operator owns B2B test cards.
- [ ] B2C Channel Operator owns B2C test cards.
- [ ] Content Engine owns asset backlog.
- [ ] Creator/Partner Manager owns creator, partner, sponsor, affiliate, and rights queues.
- [ ] Outbound/SDR Operator owns source, suppression, sender, and approval plans.
- [ ] Paid Media Operator owns creative ledger and paid-read windows.
- [ ] Analytics Operator owns dashboard, events, UTMs, and decision log.
- [ ] Compliance Reviewer owns blocked live actions and policy gaps.

## Evidence And Provenance

- [ ] `_data-sources.md` lists trusted files, user-provided data, public sources, and source dates where applicable.
- [ ] `_data-gaps.md` lists missing analytics, CRM data, proof, policies, budgets, and approvals.
- [ ] Quantitative/client-facing claims have source, retrieval/export date, evidence tier, and confidence label.
- [ ] Hypotheses are labeled as hypotheses.
- [ ] No review counts, rankings, traffic, conversions, calls, backlinks, AI-search visibility, ad metrics, or revenue claims are invented.

## Compliance

- [ ] Social automation rules are loaded before platform-bound organic execution.
- [ ] Advertising compliance and platform policy references are loaded before ad, creator, sponsorship, affiliate, or paid-social work.
- [ ] Cold email rules are loaded before outbound copy.
- [ ] Suppression and opt-out paths are defined before outbound or lifecycle sends.
- [ ] Creator briefs include FTC disclosure, affiliate disclosure when applicable, usage rights, AI/synthetic-media status, claim controls, and expiration dates.
- [ ] Paid media plans include consent, pixel/CAPI or conversion tracking status, exclusions, and claim substantiation.
- [ ] Event and sponsorship plans include consent and disclosure requirements.

## Gates

- [ ] Banned-word check passes on customer-facing markdown.
- [ ] Four U's score meets the correct threshold.
- [ ] SEO lint runs for SEO/AEO pages.
- [ ] Agent-readiness lint runs before AEO/surround-sound execution.
- [ ] Audit provenance lint runs for audit-like reports.
- [ ] The relevant skill contract is applied manually or by runner.

## Live-Action Blockers

- [ ] No email, DM, SMS, call, ad upload, spend change, post, publish action, enrichment run, CRM mutation, creator contract, or live-site edit occurs without explicit approval.
- [ ] `_quality-report.md` names every blocked live action.
- [ ] `_decision-log.md` records what was approved, rejected, or deferred.
