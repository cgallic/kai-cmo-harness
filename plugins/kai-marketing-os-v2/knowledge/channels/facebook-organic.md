# Facebook Organic Channel Guide

> **Use when:** Planning Facebook Page, Group, Reels, public post, or Messenger-adjacent organic content.

Primary references:
- Meta Community Standards: https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/
- Meta ranking explainers: https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/
- Facebook Feed AI system: https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-feed/
- Facebook recommendations: https://www.facebook.com/help/1257205004624246
- Meta organic rules: `harness/references/meta-organic-posting-rules.md`

## Channel Role

Use Facebook for community trust, local/community distribution, Groups, Reels, events, and public posts that can be retrieved or recommended across Meta surfaces.

## Distribution Notes

- Feed ranking uses viewer relationship, predicted engagement, content quality, freshness, and integrity signals.
- Recommendations can be narrower than follower delivery. A post can be allowed but not broadly recommended.
- Groups require group-specific norms. Do not treat a Group as a Page broadcast surface.
- Reels need native vertical video, a fast hook, clear captions, and original value.
- Public posts can become AI/retrieval material across Meta surfaces, so claims need source hygiene.

## Guardrails

- Load `meta-organic-posting-rules.md` before drafting or scheduling.
- Use Meta-approved APIs/tools only.
- Disclose branded content, sponsorship, affiliate incentives, and Kai ownership.
- Check Community Notes risk for factual, political, health, finance, or public-interest claims.
- Check ad rules before boosting.
