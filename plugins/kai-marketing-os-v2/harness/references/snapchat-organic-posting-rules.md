# Snapchat Organic Posting Rules

Last researched: 2026-06-17

Primary sources:
- Terms of Service: https://www.snap.com/terms
- Community Guidelines: https://values.snap.com/policy/policy-community-guidelines
- Content Guidelines for Recommendation Eligibility: https://values.snap.com/policy/content-guidelines-recommendation-eligibility
- Commercial Content Policy: https://values.snap.com/policy/content-guidelines-recommendation-eligibility/recommendation-eligibility/commercial-content
- Creator Monetization Policy: https://values.snap.com/policy/creator-monetization-policy
- Advertising Policies: https://values.snap.com/policy/advertising-policies

## Required Checks

- All public and private content must follow Snap Terms and Community Guidelines.
- Content intended to reach beyond friends/followers must satisfy Recommendation Eligibility.
- Sponsored, incentivized, brand, product, or self-promotional content must follow Commercial Content Policy.
- Use paid partnership, age, and location tools where required.
- Check Advertising Policies before paid promotion or regulated commercial content.

## Organic Distribution Guidance

- Public distribution depends on original, authentic, safe, high-quality content that viewers spend time with.
- Avoid recommendation-ineligible topics, misleading commercial content, undisclosed sponsorships, low-originality reposts, and regulated goods outside approved channels.
- For Spotlight/public content, optimize for fast visual comprehension, mobile-native vertical format, and watch quality.

## Harness Guardrails

- Load this file for Snapchat Stories, Spotlight, creator scripts, sponsored creator content, and public profile content.
- Treat any organic brand mention or product push as commercial unless clearly editorial and uncompensated.
- Do not recommend regulated goods promotion unless account eligibility and location/age controls are confirmed.
