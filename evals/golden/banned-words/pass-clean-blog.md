# How a Two-Person HVAC Shop Books 31% More Calls After Hours

Meta: Mesa HVAC contractor Rodriguez Air went from 19 missed after-hours calls a week to 3. Here is the exact call-handling setup, with costs and the 60-day numbers.

Rodriguez Air missed 19 calls a week between 6pm and 8am. Each missed emergency call in Mesa averages $410 in lost first-visit revenue. That is $7,790 a month walking to the next listing on Google.

## The 60-day fix

The shop changed three things:

1. Route after-hours calls to an answering layer with a 2-ring pickup target.
2. Tag every call with source, urgency, and zip code before the morning standup.
3. Call back every non-emergency lead before 8:30am — the callback window where their close rate held at 64%.

By day 60, missed after-hours calls dropped from 19 to 3 per week. Booked jobs from after-hours callers rose 31%.

## What to copy this week

Pull your call log for the last 30 days. Count calls outside business hours. Multiply by your average first-visit ticket. If that number clears $2,000 a month, the fix above pays for itself in the first week.
