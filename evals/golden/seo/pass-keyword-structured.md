# AI Receptionist Cost in 2026: What 5 Real Setups Pay Per Month

Meta: AI receptionist pricing ranges from $49 to $400 per month in 2026. Compare 5 real setups by call volume, with the break-even math for a phone-led local business.

An AI receptionist costs between $49 and $400 per month in 2026. The price depends on call volume, integrations, and whether you need industry-specific call scripts. This guide compares five real setups so you can match the tier to your call log.

## What an AI receptionist costs at each tier

Pricing clusters into three tiers. Match the tier to your monthly call volume before you compare vendors.

- **Starter ($49-$99/mo)**: up to 200 calls, basic message-taking, [calendar booking](/features/booking).
- **Growth ($150-$250/mo)**: up to 800 calls, CRM logging, qualification questions.
- **Pro ($300-$400/mo)**: unlimited calls, custom routing, [call analytics](/features/analytics).

## How to calculate your break-even

Pull last month's call log first. Count the calls you missed. Multiply missed calls by your average ticket and close rate. An AI receptionist pays for itself if that number beats the monthly fee. A plumber with 22 missed calls, a $310 average ticket, and a 40% close rate loses $2,728 a month — 27 times the Starter tier fee.

## Which setup fits your call volume

Pick Starter if you miss fewer than 30 calls a month. Pick Growth if your team already uses a CRM and you want every call logged. Pick Pro if you route calls across multiple locations or techs.
