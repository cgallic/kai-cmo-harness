# B2B Distribution Playbook

> **Use when:** Sequencing the full channel stack for a B2B company going from $0 to $5M ARR. This is the motion-specific layer under `playbooks/growth-distribution-engine.md` — read the master first for channel-agnostic strategy, then use this file to pick, order, and stack B2B channels.

---

## Thesis

**The first growth hire hacks distribution, not the product.** A B2B company gets to $5M ARR by finding 2-3 channels that compound, sequencing them so each one warms the next, and refusing to spread thin. Most teams die from channel sprawl, not channel scarcity.

B2B distribution runs on **trust transfer**. A buyer rarely converts on first touch. They see founder content, then get a warm outbound note, then meet you at an event, then read your AEO answer in ChatGPT, then book a demo. **The channels are not independent — they are a relay.** Your job is to build the relay in the right order for your ACV and sales motion.

Two motions split the playbook:

- **PLG / low-ACV** ($0-$5K ACV, self-serve or light-touch): demand spreads through search, communities, and content. Volume matters. Outbound is too expensive per dollar.
- **Sales-led / high-ACV** ($25K+ ACV, multi-stakeholder): demand spreads through founder credibility, targeted outbound, events, and ABM. Precision matters. Broad content is slower to pay back.

Mid-ACV ($5K-$25K) blends both. Pick by where your buyers already gather and how they already decide.

## Primary Channels — How Each Works

### LinkedIn organic / founder-led
**What it is:** The founder (and early team) posting daily insight, point-of-view, and customer proof on LinkedIn. The single highest-impact B2B channel under $5M ARR because the founder already holds the credibility buyers want.

- **Right first channel when:** founder has domain authority or a strong POV, ACV is $10K+, and buyers are on LinkedIn (most B2B). Start here by default for sales-led motions.
- **Time-to-signal:** 2-6 weeks to read whether posts earn ICP engagement (comments from buyers, not peers).
- **Cost:** low — 30-60 min/day of founder time. No ad spend.
- **How it compounds:** each post grows a warm audience that outbound, events, and webinars then convert. Followers become a reusable distribution list. Comments seed outbound conversations.
- **Deep guide:** `channels/linkedin-organic.md`. Long-form articles: `channels/linkedin-articles.md`.

### AI outbound
**What it is:** Targeted, research-backed outbound (email + LinkedIn) where AI handles list-building, account research, and first-draft personalization — but a human owns the offer and the relationship.

- **Right first channel when:** ACV is $15K+, the ICP is nameable and findable, and the founder can take sales calls. Fastest path to first revenue for sales-led startups.
- **Time-to-signal:** 1-3 weeks. Send 150-300 well-researched touches and read reply quality.
- **Cost:** medium — data tools, sending infrastructure, and warmup. Budget for deliverability, not just volume.
- **How it compounds:** warm replies feed case studies; case studies feed content; content warms the next outbound batch. Outbound also tells you exactly which message resonates — feeding every other channel.
- **Deep guide:** `channels/ai-outbound.md`. Compliance floor: `harness/references/cold-email-rules.md`.

> **Warmth rule:** Run outbound INTO an audience that already saw your founder content when possible. A cold note to someone who follows you converts far better than a true cold touch. Sequence founder content first if the timeline allows.

### Events & field marketing
**What it is:** Conferences, hosted dinners, roadshows, and small curated gatherings where buyers meet you in person. The trust accelerant for high-ACV deals.

- **Right first channel when:** ACV is $25K+, deals need multiple stakeholders, and your category already has a calendar of industry events. Rarely the literal first channel — it's the closer.
- **Time-to-signal:** one event cycle (4-8 weeks including follow-up). Read pipeline created, not badges scanned.
- **Cost:** high — sponsorship, travel, dinner costs. Hosted dinners (8-12 buyers) often beat large-booth spend per dollar.
- **How it compounds:** in-person trust shortens sales cycles and creates referenceable relationships. Event content (talks, panels) becomes LinkedIn and YouTube fuel afterward.
- **Deep guide:** `channels/events-experiential.md`. Sponsorship mechanics: `channels/sponsorships.md`.

### AEO (answer-engine optimization)
**What it is:** Structuring content and entity signals so ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Copilot cite you when buyers ask category questions.

- **Right first channel when:** buyers research independently before contacting sales (most B2B in 2026), and your category has clear question patterns. Compounds for all ACVs.
- **Time-to-signal:** 4-12 weeks to start appearing in answer engines for target prompts.
- **Cost:** low-medium — overlaps with content production. The structural work (entity home, schema, atomic facts) is mostly one-time.
- **How it compounds:** answer-engine citations create demand you never pay per-click for, and the citation itself is third-party-feeling trust. Compounds with every new authoritative page.
- **Deep guide:** `frameworks/aeo-ai-search/aeo-ai-search-playbook-2026.md`. Gate AEO/site work through `scripts/quality_gates/agent_readiness_lint.py`.

### Blogs / SEO content
**What it is:** Educational articles targeting problem-aware and solution-aware search queries. The classic compounding asset, still the floor under AEO.

- **Right first channel when:** the product is PLG/self-serve, buyers search for the problem, and you can wait 3-6 months for payback. The default first channel for low-ACV PLG.
- **Time-to-signal:** 3-6 months before rankings compound into pipeline. Slowest to start, highest ceiling.
- **Cost:** low-medium per article; the cost is patience and consistency (2-4 posts/week to build momentum).
- **How it compounds:** ranked pages keep producing pipeline at near-zero marginal cost. Each post strengthens topical authority, lifting the next. Doubles as AEO substrate.
- **Deep guides:** `channels/seo-content.md`, `channels/content-writing.md`. Frameworks: `knowledge/frameworks/content-copywriting/algorithmic-authorship.md`. Checklist: `knowledge/checklists/seo-checklist.md`.

### Long-form writing / expert authority
**What it is:** Substantive POV essays, LinkedIn articles, and newsletters that take a defensible position on your category.

- **Right first channel when:** the founder has a contrarian, evidenced POV and the ACV justifies trust-heavy selling ($10K+).
- **Time-to-signal:** 1-3 months. Read whether the right buyers reference your ideas, not just read counts.
- **Cost:** low in dollars, high in thinking time. One strong essay can outpull a month of short posts.
- **How it compounds:** a signature piece becomes the asset every other channel points to — the thing outbound links, the talk you give at events, the answer engines cite.
- **Deep guide:** `channels/linkedin-articles.md`. X long-form: `channels/x-longform.md`.

### YouTube
**What it is:** Long-form video (tutorials, teardowns, customer stories) plus Shorts. The highest-trust, most evergreen video channel.

- **Right first channel when:** the product benefits from demonstration, or buyers search "how to {problem}" on YouTube. Stronger as a second/third channel for most B2B.
- **Time-to-signal:** 3-9 months. Video ranks for years but takes time to compound.
- **Cost:** medium — production time and a basic kit. Repurpose talks and webinars to lower the cost.
- **How it compounds:** videos rank in Google and YouTube, get cited in AI Overviews, and build founder trust that lifts every other channel. Evergreen tutorials become sales-assist assets.
- **Deep guide:** `channels/youtube.md`.

### Webinars
**What it is:** Live or evergreen sessions teaching the ICP something useful, with a soft path to demo.

- **Right first channel when:** ACV is $10K+ and you have an audience (even small) to invite. Works fast as a demand-capture layer, not as a cold-start channel.
- **Time-to-signal:** 2-4 weeks from promote to pipeline.
- **Cost:** low-medium. The cost is the audience to fill the room — co-host to borrow one.
- **How it compounds:** recordings become evergreen content; registrant lists feed nurture and outbound; co-hosting opens partner audiences.
- **Deep guide:** `playbooks/event-webinar-marketing.md`.

### X / Twitter
**What it is:** Short-form posting and long-form threads, strongest for developer-tool, technical, and founder-audience categories.

- **Right first channel when:** you sell to developers, founders, or technical buyers who live on X. Weaker for traditional enterprise.
- **Time-to-signal:** 1-3 months to build a reactive audience.
- **Cost:** low (time). Replies and threads cost attention, not dollars.
- **How it compounds:** a technical audience compounds into community, beta users, and inbound. Threads repurpose into blog posts and LinkedIn.
- **Deep guides:** `channels/x-twitter.md`, `channels/x-longform.md`.

### B2B influencers / creators
**What it is:** Paid or partnered placements with creators who already hold your ICP's attention (newsletter writers, podcasters, LinkedIn voices).

- **Right first channel when:** your category has trusted independent creators and you need to borrow audience fast. A speed lever, not a foundation.
- **Time-to-signal:** 2-8 weeks per placement.
- **Cost:** medium-high per placement; price varies widely by audience size and fit.
- **How it compounds:** repeated placements with the same creator build associative trust. Creator content becomes social proof you reuse.
- **Deep guide:** `playbooks/influencer-marketing.md`.

## Pick Your First 2-3 Channels

Choose by ACV and motion. Commit for 90 days before judging. Do not start more than three.

### PLG / low-ACV ($0-$5K, self-serve)

```
PRIMARY    SEO content + AEO     (compounding inbound; buyers search the problem)
SECONDARY  Communities           (free demand where ICP already gathers)
THIRD      Founder LinkedIn      (trust + a reusable audience)
```

Avoid heavy outbound and events early — CAC per self-serve dollar rarely works.

### Sales-led / high-ACV ($25K+, multi-stakeholder)

```
PRIMARY    Founder LinkedIn      (credibility the buyer wants)
SECONDARY  AI outbound           (fastest path to first revenue)
THIRD      Events / webinars     (in-person trust closes large deals)
```

Add ABM once you have 3-5 reference customers and a nameable target list.

### Mid-ACV ($5K-$25K, light-touch sales)

```
PRIMARY    Founder LinkedIn      (audience + warmth)
SECONDARY  AI outbound (warmed)  (convert the audience you build)
THIRD      AEO + content         (compounding inbound that lowers CAC over time)
```

### Quick selector

| If your buyer... | Start with |
|------------------|-----------|
| Searches for the problem before talking to anyone | SEO content + AEO |
| Is nameable, findable, and worth $15K+ | AI outbound + founder LinkedIn |
| Decides by committee on $50K+ deals | Founder LinkedIn + events + ABM |
| Lives in a specific community or on X | That community + X |
| Trusts independent creators in your space | Founder LinkedIn + B2B creators |

## How Channels Stack Into a System

Channels relay trust. **Build the relay, not a pile of disconnected tactics.**

```
FOUNDER CONTENT  →  builds a warm ICP audience + sharpens the message
       │
       ▼
OUTBOUND WARMUP  →  reaches that audience with a precise offer; replies become proof
       │
       ▼
EVENTS / WEBINARS →  convert warm relationships in person; create reference customers
       │
       ▼
AEO / SEO / YOUTUBE → compounding inbound cites your proof; lowers CAC over time
       │
       ▼
PARTNERSHIPS / ABM → concentrate the working system on the best accounts
       │
       ▼
PAID → amplify only the messages and offers already proven above
```

Each channel feeds the next:

- **Content → outbound:** outbound into people who already saw your posts converts far better than cold.
- **Outbound → content:** every reply tells you which pain and phrasing land. Feed it back into content.
- **Events → content:** talks and panels become LinkedIn posts, YouTube videos, and AEO pages.
- **Content → AEO:** authoritative pages are what answer engines cite.
- **Everything → paid:** paid scales a proven message; it cannot create one.

## Sequenced Build Order

### $0 → $1M ARR (find the first 2 channels that work)

```
WEEKS 1-4    Founder publishes daily on LinkedIn. Define ICP + sharp POV.
             Stand up AEO basics (entity home, schema, robots/llms.txt).
WEEKS 3-8    Launch AI outbound: 150-300 researched touches/week into ICP.
             Warm where possible (people who engaged with founder content).
WEEKS 6-12   Convert replies to calls. Ship first 3-5 case studies.
             Start 1 content lane (SEO if PLG, thought-leadership if sales-led).
WEEKS 10-16  Run first webinar or hosted dinner from the warm list.
             Read which channel produces the most qualified pipeline.
WEEKS 16-24  Double down on the single best channel. Add ONE second channel.
             Cut anything not producing qualified pipeline. Document the playbook.
```

**Exit test:** one channel reliably produces qualified pipeline, CAC and payback are known, and another operator could repeat the motion. (See `playbooks/marketing-by-stage.md` Early→Growth checklist.)

### $1M → $5M ARR (systematize and add compounding inbound)

```
QUARTER 1    Systematize the winning channel. Hire your first growth person.
             Add retargeting (cheapest paid) on content engagers + demo visitors.
QUARTER 2    Scale the content/AEO engine (2-4 pieces/week). It compounds toward CAC drop.
             Launch partnerships / co-marketing for borrowed audience.
QUARTER 3    Add events cadence (sponsor + host dinners) if ACV is $25K+.
             Begin ABM on a named target-account list with reference customers.
QUARTER 4    Build a 3-channel portfolio. Add YouTube or B2B creators for top-of-funnel.
             Layer paid to amplify proven messages. Cut underperformers.
```

**Exit test:** 2-3 channels produce reliably, branded/inbound demand is growing, no single channel is >60% of pipeline. (See Growth→Scale checklist.)

## Weekly Operating Cadence

```
MONDAY     Review last week: pipeline sourced/influenced by channel, CAC, reply rates.
           Set the week's content angle from the best outbound reply or sales call.
TUE-THU    Founder publishes (LinkedIn daily). Send outbound batch.
           Ship 1 content piece. Advance event/webinar prep.
FRIDAY     Read signals: which channel produced qualified pipeline this week?
           Log winners to `knowledge/playbooks/what-works.md`; losers to `memory/`.
MONTHLY    Run /kai-retro. Re-cut budget by evidence. Promote or kill a channel.
           Verify the channel-distribution-checklist before adding anything new.
```

Run new channels and content through the quality gates before they ship: `four_us_score.py`, `banned_word_check.py`, and `seo_lint.py` for SEO. Validate the full plan against `checklists/channel-distribution-checklist.md`.

## Measurement

Measure pipeline and payback, not activity. Every quantitative client-facing claim follows the Kai Data Provenance Rule — collect first, cite the source.

| Metric | What it tells you | Evidence needed |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Marketing-sourced pipeline** | New pipeline where marketing created the first hand-raiser | CRM source + UTM rules |
| **Marketing-influenced pipeline** | Pipeline marketing touched anywhere in the buying process | Multi-touch definition + lookback window |
| **Pipeline by channel** | Which relay step actually creates qualified deals | Channel attribution rules |
| **CAC by channel** | Cost to acquire a customer per channel | Spend source + win data |
| **CAC payback (months)** | How long until a customer pays back acquisition cost | Gross margin + ACV |
| **LTV:CAC** | Whether the channel mix is sustainable | Retention + margin assumptions |
| **Reply / meeting rate (outbound)** | Outbound message-market fit | Send logs + qualification rule |
| **Sales-cycle length by channel** | Which channels shorten the path to close | Stage-timestamp hygiene |

Targets are local calibration, not universal truth. A healthy B2B benchmark range is **CAC payback under 12-18 months** and **LTV:CAC above 3:1** — but replace these with the company's real economics before any recommendation.

## Failure Modes

| Failure | What it looks like | Fix |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| **Channel sprawl** | Five half-run channels, none producing | Cut to 2-3. Commit 90 days each. |
| **Cold outbound with no warmup** | Low reply rates, burned domains | Run founder content first; warm the list. |
| **Content with no distribution** | Publishing into the void | Pair every piece with active distribution (post, send, syndicate). |
| **Events as the first channel** | High spend, no pipeline, no follow-up | Events close warm relationships — build warmth first. |
| **Paid before message-market fit** | Spend scales losses | Find the converting message organically, then amplify with paid. |
| **Vanity metrics** | Followers and impressions up, pipeline flat | Measure ICP engagement and sourced pipeline, not reach. |
| **No relay** | Channels run in isolation | Connect them: content→outbound→events→inbound. |
| **Abandoning compounding channels early** | Killing SEO/AEO at month 2 | These pay back at month 4-6. Hold the commitment. |
| **Single-channel dependency** | 80%+ pipeline from one channel | Add a second channel before the first saturates. |
