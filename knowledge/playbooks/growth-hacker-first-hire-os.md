# Growth Hacker First-Hire OS

> **Use when:** A founder needs the first distribution hire, a solo growth operator needs a channel map, or Kai needs to turn B2B/B2C channel ideas into an executable 90-day system.

This playbook turns the "growth hacker first hire" into an operator room. The hire is not a generic marketer. The hire owns channel discovery, fast tests, proof capture, creative volume, partner surface area, and the measurement loop that decides what graduates.

## Core Thesis

The first growth hire should find distribution before the company builds a large marketing team. The job is to run channel probes, find one or two repeatable acquisition loops, then turn those loops into systems other people can run.

The target is not "do every channel forever." The target is:

1. Map every plausible channel.
2. Pick the channels with the strongest stage fit.
3. Run small tests with clean source tracking.
4. Kill weak channels quickly.
5. Scale channels only after the proof, offer, landing page, and follow-up path work together.

## Operating Principles

- **One owner, many specialists.** The growth hacker owns the scoreboard and uses specialist workers for content, outbound, creators, paid media, analytics, and compliance.
- **Distribution before polish.** Ship tests with enough quality to protect trust, then improve what shows signal.
- **Proof before volume.** Do not scale content, spend, outbound, or creator programs without a proof source and a next-step path.
- **Owned loop before rented reach.** Paid, creators, events, and social are stronger when they feed email, community, search assets, CRM, and retargeting pools.
- **Compliance is part of speed.** A blocked account, spam complaint spike, missing disclosure, or live-channel mutation without approval is not a growth win.

## Stage Fit

| Stage | Growth Hacker Job | Channel Bias | What To Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch | Find painful demand and language | Founder LinkedIn, interviews, communities, waitlist page, manual outreach | Paid scale, huge content calendars, broad positioning |
| First revenue | Find one repeatable hand-raiser path | Outbound tests, niche content, webinars, founder social, referrals | Hiring agencies before the offer converts |
| Early growth | Systematize the first channel and test two more | SEO/AEO, lifecycle, retargeting, partner webinars, creator tests | Running every channel with no owner |
| Growth | Build channel portfolio and operating cadence | ABM, paid media, YouTube, events, influencer, newsletter, co-marketing | Treating platform attribution as profit |
| Scale | Turn winners into teams, budgets, and memory | Specialist ownership, dashboards, playbooks, partner programs | Founder-only distribution bottlenecks |

## B2B Distribution Map

### LinkedIn Organic

**Use when:** The buyer is reachable through founders, operators, executives, recruiters, consultants, or niche professional communities.

**Inputs:** ICP, point of view, proof assets, founder voice, target accounts, event calendar, employee handles.

**Execution loop:**
1. Publish founder/operator posts three to five times per week.
2. Mix field notes, proof, teardown, objection handling, and event commentary.
3. Reply to high-fit conversations daily.
4. Turn comments, objections, and DMs into the next content brief.
5. Route warm replies to `/kai-sdr-operator` or `/kai-sales-meeting-prep`.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-social`, `/kai-write`, `/kai-repurpose`, `/kai-analytics`, `/kai-gate`.

**Gates:** LinkedIn organic rules, no engagement pods, no fake employee advocacy, source every claim, no copy-paste employee posts.

**Metrics:** Qualified comments, saves, profile clicks, target-account engagement, booked calls, influenced opportunities.

**Anti-patterns:** Link-only posting, generic executive posts, private customer details, product posts over the content mix.

### LinkedIn Articles And Newsletters

**Use when:** The company needs durable founder authority, category education, or executive-level explanation.

**Inputs:** Keyword/entity target, proof packet, original thesis, customer language, visual examples.

**Execution loop:**
1. Publish one or two high-quality articles per month.
2. Promote each article with three short posts and one founder comment thread.
3. Repurpose the article into email, X, short video, and sales enablement.
4. Refresh the article when field data changes.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-write`, `/kai-content-calendar`, `/kai-repurpose`, `/kai-gate`.

**Gates:** `linkedin-article.yaml`, Four U's 12/16, banned-word check, LinkedIn feed-quality checklist, provenance for numbers.

**Metrics:** Reads, subscribers, saves, comments, sales-team usage, assisted pipeline.

**Anti-patterns:** Dense essays with no field evidence, external links before the main point, vague future-of-work language.

### AI Outbound And SDR

**Use when:** There is a clear ICP, sourceable account list, reachable buyer, and a high-value next step.

**Inputs:** Lead source plan, suppression list, lawful basis, sender setup, account scorecard, trigger evidence, CRM rules.

**Execution loop:**
1. Write `_lead-source-plan.md` before gathering contacts.
2. Score accounts by fit, timing, pain evidence, reachability, and compliance confidence.
3. Suppress before copy.
4. Send only an approved test cohort.
5. Triage replies, prep meetings, log objections, and update the source-quality memory.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-sdr-operator`, `/kai-cold-outreach`, `/kai-sdr-reply-triage`, `/kai-sales-meeting-prep`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** cold email rules, suppression, opt-out path, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, no bought lists, approval before live send or CRM mutation.

**Metrics:** Positive replies, meetings, bounce rate, complaints, source quality, opportunity creation, sales acceptance.

**Anti-patterns:** Bulk generic copy, unsupported personalization, scraping that violates terms, missing opt-out, sending before suppression.

### AEO And AI Search

**Use when:** Buyers ask Google AI Search, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Bing/Copilot, or Grok for category answers, comparisons, or vendor shortlists.

**Inputs:** Entity home, crawl policy, schema, prompt set, source-quality map, third-party corroboration opportunities.

**Execution loop:**
1. Run agent-readiness checks before surround-sound work.
2. Clean the entity home, Organization schema, author bios, citations, and source pages.
3. Publish atomic answer assets and comparison pages with evidence.
4. Build source-quality mentions through PR, podcasts, partner pages, directories, and expert content.
5. Sample engines repeatedly and log answer accuracy, citations, and gaps.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-surround-sound`, `/kai-topical-map`, `/kai-seo-audit`, `/kai-write`, `/kai-gate`.

**Gates:** `agent_readiness_lint.py`, audit provenance, no ranking/citation guarantees, no hidden ownership, no fabricated consensus.

**Metrics:** Citation presence, answer accuracy, AI referrals, branded search, source-quality growth, qualified visits.

**Anti-patterns:** Treating `llms.txt` as a Google ranking factor, fake review sites, thin listicles, unverifiable claims.

### Blogs, SEO, And Long-Form Writing

**Use when:** Buyers search problems, comparisons, methods, alternatives, or implementation steps.

**Inputs:** Topic map, keyword intent, SERP/PAA notes, proof packet, internal links, persona, conversion path.

**Execution loop:**
1. Build a topical map before producing volume.
2. Write briefs with search intent, answer blocks, internal links, and proof requirements.
3. Publish problem-aware, answer-aware, and comparison content.
4. Refresh from Search Console, CRM source data, and sales objections.
5. Atomize each winning piece into LinkedIn, X, newsletter, and video scripts.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-topical-map`, `/kai-brief`, `/kai-write`, `/kai-content-calendar`, `/kai-repurpose`, `/kai-gate`.

**Gates:** blog contract, Four U's 12/16, banned-word check, SEO lint, source tier for claims.

**Metrics:** Impressions, clicks, qualified visits, assisted pipeline, demo page assists, internal-link coverage.

**Anti-patterns:** Commodity rewrites, doorway pages, claim stuffing, writing before offer clarity.

### YouTube

**Use when:** The category needs education, demos, teardown content, tutorials, or searchable long-form trust.

**Inputs:** YouTube topic map, title/thumbnail hypothesis, script, product footage, chapters, captions, disclosure fields.

**Execution loop:**
1. Publish one durable long-form video per week or every other week.
2. Cut each video into Shorts, LinkedIn clips, X clips, and newsletter sections.
3. Track title/thumbnail CTR, retention, comments, and lead paths.
4. Turn comments into product docs, content briefs, and sales objections.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-video`, `/kai-video-production`, `/kai-repurpose`, `/kai-social`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** YouTube organic rules, AI disclosure when applicable, paid-promo fields, COPPA checks when relevant, caption QA.

**Metrics:** CTR, retention, watch time, subscribers, qualified traffic, assisted conversions.

**Anti-patterns:** Long intros, misleading thumbnails, unedited webinar dumps, repetitive AI-generated scripts.

### Webinars And Events

**Use when:** The buyer needs education, the sales cycle needs trust, partners can bring an audience, or the company needs live hand-raisers.

**Inputs:** ICP pain, topic, speaker, partner, registration path, follow-up CTA, consent rules.

**Execution loop:**
1. Validate the topic against audience pain and differentiation.
2. Promote for four weeks across email, LinkedIn, X, partners, and paid retargeting.
3. Deliver live value, collect questions, record, and segment attendees.
4. Follow up by attendance, engagement, and account fit.
5. Repurpose into blog, clips, newsletter, sales enablement, and AEO sources.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-webinar`, `/kai-email-system`, `/kai-social`, `/kai-repurpose`, `/kai-partnership`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** event/webinar checklist, email gates, consent for shared leads, one CTA per asset, claim support.

**Metrics:** Registrations, attendance, engagement, watch time, qualified meetings, partner-sourced pipeline.

**Anti-patterns:** Product-pitch topics, no follow-up segmentation, no recording plan, no partner tracking.

### X And Founder Media

**Use when:** The category has live discourse, founder-led trust matters, or ideas need rapid market feedback.

**Inputs:** Founder voice, proof, topics, examples, reply targets, event/calendar hooks.

**Execution loop:**
1. Post point-of-view, proof, teardown, and build-in-public updates.
2. Reply daily to accounts that gather the target audience.
3. Turn top posts into LinkedIn, newsletter, and blog briefs.
4. Route high-intent replies to sales only when the person shows direct interest.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-social`, `/kai-write`, `/kai-repurpose`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** X organic rules, automation guardrails, no duplicate posting, claim support, promoted-post policy checks when paid.

**Metrics:** Replies, reposts, profile clicks, qualified DMs, follower quality, site traffic.

**Anti-patterns:** Auto-replies, hashtag blocks, rage bait, copy-pasted threads, links with no native value.

### B2B Influencers And Creators

**Use when:** Niche trust, analyst-style explanation, practitioner demos, or creator-led UGC can shorten the trust path.

**Inputs:** Creator fit matrix, audience proof, brief, claim controls, usage rights, disclosure plan, economics.

**Execution loop:**
1. Build a shortlist by audience fit and content quality.
2. Brief creators on talking points, not scripts.
3. Approve claims, disclosures, and usage rights before publication.
4. Track each creator by UTMs, codes, sales notes, and asset reuse value.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-influencer`, `/kai-ad-campaign`, `/kai-partnership`, `/kai-gate`.

**Gates:** FTC disclosure, usage rights, affiliate disclosure, AI/synthetic media labels, claim substantiation.

**Metrics:** Creator-level reach, qualified traffic, code/link use, sales notes, reusable asset count, compliance status.

**Anti-patterns:** Hidden sponsorships, expired-rights reuse, macro reach with no buyer fit, unsupported superlatives.

### Partnerships, Co-Marketing, And Sponsorships

**Use when:** A partner already owns trust with the audience or the product connects naturally to another workflow.

**Inputs:** Partner fit matrix, mutual value, audience overlap, lead-sharing consent, offer, tracking plan.

**Execution loop:**
1. Pitch one specific co-marketing asset, not a vague partnership.
2. Co-create a webinar, report, integration page, template, or event.
3. Give both sides a promotion kit.
4. Track partner source and sales acceptance.
5. Decide whether to repeat, expand, or stop.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-partnership`, `/kai-webinar`, `/kai-newsletter`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** consent for shared leads, disclosure, UTM/CRM tracking, no channel conflict, no fake endorsement.

**Metrics:** Partner leads, pipeline, integration adoption, email list growth, source quality.

**Anti-patterns:** Logo swaps with no audience, no owner, no tracking, no follow-up plan.

## B2C Distribution Map

### AI UGC And Creative Volume

**Use when:** The offer needs many hooks, demos, creator-style testimonials, before/after-safe alternatives, or paid social creative.

**Inputs:** Offer, audience, product footage, claim file, creator persona, usage rights, disclosure needs, creative ledger.

**Execution loop:**
1. Generate hook and concept batches from real customer language.
2. Produce AI-assisted scripts and storyboards.
3. Use humans or approved synthetic-media workflows for final assets.
4. Log each asset by concept, proof type, AI status, rights, disclosure, landing match, and kill rule.
5. Feed winners into paid social, TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and landing pages.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-influencer`, `/kai-ad-campaign`, `/kai-video-production`, `/kai-social`, `/kai-gate`.

**Gates:** platform AI disclosure, synthetic-media rights, FTC disclosure, paid usage rights, claim support, no deceptive testimonials.

**Metrics:** Hook rate, hold rate, watch-through, thumb-stop, saves, clicks, CPA, asset reuse.

**Anti-patterns:** Fake customer stories, undisclosed AI avatars, editing creator content outside rights, claim-heavy scripts with no proof.

### Organic TikTok

**Use when:** The audience learns through short-form video, culture, demos, transformation stories, or entertainment-led education.

**Inputs:** Audience jobs-to-be-done, creator angle, trend library, product demos, comment mining, disclosure rules.

**Execution loop:**
1. Post daily when TikTok is a priority channel.
2. Test hooks, formats, creator personas, and proof angles.
3. Reply to comments with new videos.
4. Move winners into paid social only after rights and landing match pass.
5. Feed repeated questions into SEO, email, and product education.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-social`, `/kai-video`, `/kai-video-production`, `/kai-repurpose`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** TikTok organic rules, music rights, AI disclosure, branded-content disclosure, no sensitive-attribute claims.

**Metrics:** Completion rate, rewatch, shares, profile visits, follower quality, click-through, assisted sales.

**Anti-patterns:** Trend-chasing with no brand memory, hidden sponsorship, posting only product demos, unlicensed music in ads.

### Paid Social

**Use when:** The funnel, tracking, offer, and creative bench are strong enough to buy learning.

**Inputs:** Pixel/CAPI state, consent, offer, landing page, creative ledger, policy category, budget, read window.

**Execution loop:**
1. Confirm tracking, consent, events, and exclusions before launch.
2. Build creative batches by concept, not minor edits.
3. Start with a clear optimization event and named kill rules.
4. Read performance by creative concept, landing match, margin, and audience state.
5. Scale winners only after the economics and measurement confidence are clear.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-ad-campaign`, `/kai-retarget`, `/kai-daily-ad-review`, `/kai-analytics`, `/kai-landing-page`.

**Gates:** platform policy pack, tracking QA, consent, claim substantiation, creative-rights ledger.

**Metrics:** CPA, CAC, contribution margin, creative fatigue, frequency, incremental read quality, refund-adjusted sales.

**Anti-patterns:** Reporting platform ROAS as profit, weak event data, no exclusions, scaling one winning hook until fatigue.

### B2C Influencers And Creator Commerce

**Use when:** The product benefits from social proof, demos, lifestyle context, niche trust, or affiliate-style distribution.

**Inputs:** SKU economics, creator tiers, audience proof, brief, product sample plan, affiliate terms, usage rights.

**Execution loop:**
1. Seed or sponsor small creators before large contracts.
2. Track creator assets separately from creator sales.
3. Whitelist or reuse only assets with explicit paid usage rights.
4. Compare creator results to margin, refund, and baseline demand.
5. Promote repeat winners into ambassador or affiliate programs.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-influencer`, `/kai-ad-campaign`, `/kai-retarget`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** FTC disclosure, affiliate disclosure, usage rights, AI/synthetic media terms, SKU margin checks.

**Metrics:** Creator-level clicks, code use, contribution margin, usable assets, paid-boost performance, repeat purchase.

**Anti-patterns:** Paying for follower count, ignoring refund data, no disclosure capture, using creator footage after rights expire.

### Events, Pop-Ups, And Field Marketing

**Use when:** The product is experiential, local, community-driven, premium, or needs trial.

**Inputs:** Audience geography, event fit, sample inventory, staffing, capture method, post-event offer, safety/compliance notes.

**Execution loop:**
1. Pick events by audience concentration, not crowd size.
2. Use a simple on-site CTA: sample, scan, join, book, or buy.
3. Capture consent and source at the point of interaction.
4. Follow up within the stated window.
5. Turn field reactions into creative, landing page, and product notes.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-webinar`, `/kai-launch`, `/kai-social`, `/kai-email-system`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** consent, giveaway rules, local event rules, claims, photo/video release when people appear in content.

**Metrics:** Scans, trials, samples-to-sales, email/SMS opt-ins, local lift, creator/event content output.

**Anti-patterns:** Swag with no capture, no follow-up owner, untracked QR codes, filming attendees without consent.

### Sponsorships

**Use when:** A newsletter, podcast, event, creator, community, or media property owns repeated attention from a defined audience.

**Inputs:** Audience proof, rate card, placement format, offer, landing page, tracking link, brand safety review.

**Execution loop:**
1. Start with one placement or short flight.
2. Match offer to the audience context.
3. Use dedicated landing pages or codes.
4. Review assisted lift, not direct clicks alone.
5. Repeat only if the audience, creative, and economics support it.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-partnership`, `/kai-influencer`, `/kai-ad-campaign`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** sponsorship disclosure, claims, brand safety, affiliate terms, lead-sharing consent if applicable.

**Metrics:** Reach quality, visits, branded search lift, code use, assisted conversions, cost per qualified action.

**Anti-patterns:** Buying logo placement, no landing match, one-off read with no follow-up, no audience proof.

### Email, SMS, And Retention Loops

**Use when:** The company has owned contacts, repeat purchase potential, trials, abandoned carts, usage events, or lifecycle moments.

**Inputs:** Consent source, segments, event triggers, offer, suppression, preference center, revenue events.

**Execution loop:**
1. Build welcome, abandon, nurture, post-purchase, review, referral, and winback flows.
2. Segment by behavior and lifecycle stage.
3. Test subject lines, offer sequence, send timing, and landing match.
4. Suppress inactive or risky segments.
5. Feed insights into paid, social, and product education.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-email-system`, `/kai-newsletter`, `/kai-retention`, `/kai-retarget`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** CAN-SPAM, SMS consent, one-click unsubscribe where required, suppression, claims.

**Metrics:** Revenue per recipient, conversion, complaints, unsubscribe, repeat purchase, reactivation.

**Anti-patterns:** Full-list blasts, open-rate-only decisions, weak consent records, discounting every message.

### Referral, Affiliate, And Community Loops

**Use when:** Customers naturally share results, communities trust peer recommendations, or creators/partners can earn from real sales.

**Inputs:** Referral reward, affiliate terms, fraud rules, community rules, tracking links, payout process.

**Execution loop:**
1. Define the selfish reason to share.
2. Place the ask after an activation, success, delivery, or delight moment.
3. Track referrals by source, buyer quality, and fraud risk.
4. Give partners assets they can adapt without claim risk.
5. Promote the program only after unit economics are clear.

**Kai skills:** `/kai-partnership`, `/kai-influencer`, `/kai-retention`, `/kai-email-system`, `/kai-analytics`.

**Gates:** affiliate disclosure, fraud controls, payout terms, claims, platform/community rules.

**Metrics:** Referral conversion, affiliate revenue, margin, fraud flags, partner retention.

**Anti-patterns:** Referral asks before value, vague rewards, no fraud rules, affiliates making unsupported claims.

## Channel Prioritization Score

Score each channel from 1 to 5.

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Audience density | Does the ICP gather here often enough to matter? |
| Message fit | Can the channel carry the proof, offer, and story? |
| Speed to signal | Can we learn within the sprint window? |
| Cost to test | Can we test without large sunk cost or fragile setup? |
| Compounding value | Does output become an owned asset, list, source, or loop? |
| Compliance risk | Can we run this without policy, consent, or data risk? |
| Operator advantage | Do we have unusual taste, data, access, or speed here? |

Promote channels with high audience density, strong message fit, fast signal, and compounding value. Block or delay channels with unresolved compliance risk.

## 90-Day Sprint

### Days 1-10: Inventory And Score

- Load `MARKETING.md`, current analytics, CRM/source data, and existing assets.
- Create `_channel-map.md` across B2B and B2C.
- Write `_evidence-ledger.md` and `_data-gaps.md`.
- Score every channel using the prioritization model.
- Pick one primary channel, one secondary channel, and one cheap exploratory channel.

### Days 11-30: Build Test Assets

- Create landing page or offer page if the next step is weak.
- Build the first content, outbound, paid, creator, or event assets.
- Set up UTMs, source rules, consent, and quality gates.
- Run a small test cohort or limited creative batch.
- Log every result in `_decision-log.md`.

### Days 31-60: Read And Iterate

- Kill channels with no audience fit, no source quality, or high compliance risk.
- Improve the strongest message, offer, landing page, and follow-up path.
- Repurpose winners across owned channels.
- Start a second batch only from what the first batch taught.

### Days 61-90: Graduate Or Reset

- Graduate one channel into a repeatable operating cadence.
- Write the runbook, owner map, dashboard, and budget notes.
- Keep one exploratory channel alive.
- Reset the rest with explicit kill reasons.

## Gate Before Handoff

Run or manually apply the relevant gates for every produced asset:

- `python scripts/quality_gates/banned_word_check.py --file <file>`
- `python scripts/quality_gates/four_us_score.py --file <file>`
- `python scripts/quality_gates/seo_lint.py --file <file>` for SEO/AEO pages
- `python scripts/quality_gates/audit_provenance_lint.py <folder> --audit-dir` for audit-like reports
- `python scripts/quality_gates/agent_readiness_lint.py https://<domain>` before AEO/surround-sound work

Use ad policy references before writing ad copy. Use organic posting references before scheduling or posting to social platforms. Do not send, publish, upload, spend, scrape, enrich, call, text, mutate CRM data, or change live channels without explicit approval.
