# LinkedIn AI Systems — Deep Research Plan

## Objective
Comprehensive reverse-engineering of LinkedIn's AI/ML systems: patents, research papers, engineering blogs, key personnel, algorithms, and detection systems.

## Context from User-Provided Images
- LinkedIn has developed an "AI solving AI" approach to detect AI-generated "slop" content
- Tech built in partnership with LinkedIn's editorial teams
- Human editors annotate thousands of posts, labeling generic vs. original
- Multiple reviewers per post for consistency
- Also targeting "attention-bait videos"
- Key figure: "Lorenzetti" (LinkedIn executive/researcher)

---

## Stage 1 — Multi-Angle Parallel Research (Wide Search)
Deploy 6 parallel research agents covering all dimensions:

### Agent 1: Patent & IP Researcher
- Search USPTO, Google Patents, LinkedIn patent filings for AI/ML systems
- Focus areas: content ranking, feed algorithms, AI detection, recommendation systems, job matching, skill inference
- Search terms: "LinkedIn AI patent", "LinkedIn machine learning patent", "LinkedIn content ranking patent"
- Also search Microsoft patents that involve LinkedIn technology

### Agent 2: Engineering Blog & Technical Paper Researcher
- Scrape LinkedIn Engineering blog (engineering.linkedin.com)
- Search for technical papers on arXiv, ACM, IEEE mentioning LinkedIn
- Focus: feed algorithm, AI content detection, "AI slop" detection, ranking systems
- Search LinkedIn's technical publications and conference presentations

### Agent 3: Key People & Org Structure Researcher
- Identify LinkedIn AI leadership: VP of AI, Chief Scientist, key researchers
- Find the "Lorenzetti" person quoted in the article
- Search LinkedIn's AI/ML team structure
- Identify researchers publishing on LinkedIn's AI systems
- Look at LinkedIn AI hiring patterns and team leads

### Agent 4: AI Content Detection & Moderation Systems Researcher
- Deep dive on LinkedIn's AI-generated content detection systems
- The "AI solving AI" approach described in the article
- How they detect attention-bait videos, AI comments, low-quality content
- Partnership with editorial teams — how human-in-the-loop works
- Search for LinkedIn's trust & safety AI systems

### Agent 5: Feed Algorithm & Recommendation Systems Researcher
- LinkedIn feed ranking algorithm (how posts are ranked)
- LinkedIn's "interest graph" vs. "social graph" approach
- Job recommendation AI, "People You May Know", skill recommendations
- Search for any leaked or published details about the feed algorithm
- LinkedIn's use of embeddings, neural networks, collaborative filtering

### Agent 6: LinkedIn AI Products & External Analysis Researcher
- LinkedIn's AI-powered products: collaborative articles, AI job descriptions, LinkedIn Learning recommendations
- Third-party analyses, reverse-engineering attempts, academic studies on LinkedIn's algorithm
- Search for independent researchers who've studied LinkedIn's algorithm
- Any leaked documents, API behavior analysis, or algorithm audits

---

## Stage 2 — Deep Dive & Cross-Verification
- Cross-verify findings from Stage 1
- Deep dive into the most promising leads
- Verify patent numbers, author identities, publication dates
- Fill gaps where agents reported limited findings

## Stage 3 — Synthesis & Report Writing
- Compile all findings into a comprehensive report
- Structure: Patents, Key People, Algorithms & Systems, Content Detection AI, Feed/Recommendation Systems, External Research & Reverse Engineering
- Cite all sources with URLs
- Include actionable insights about how each system works

## Skill: deep-research-swarm (Stage 1 & 2), report-writing (Stage 3)
