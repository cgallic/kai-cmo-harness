# Bluesky Organic Channel Guide

> **Use when:** Planning Bluesky posts, custom feeds, decentralized social listening, or AT Protocol publishing.

Primary references:
- Community Guidelines: https://bsky.social/about/support/community-guidelines
- Rate limits: https://docs.bsky.app/docs/advanced-guides/rate-limits
- Custom feeds: https://docs.bsky.app/docs/starter-templates/custom-feeds
- Firehose: https://docs.bsky.app/docs/advanced-guides/firehose
- API blog: https://docs.bsky.app/blog

## Channel Role

Use Bluesky for decentralized public conversation, early-adopter communities, topic feeds, technical founder POV, and custom-feed discovery.

## Distribution Notes

- Distribution can come from follows, reposts, algorithmic/custom feeds, starter packs, labels, and community norms.
- Custom feeds have their own ranking logic. Optimize for the feed context, not a single universal algorithm.
- Labels and moderation services can affect visibility and trust.

## Guardrails

- Follow community guidelines and write-rate limits.
- Clearly label bots or automated accounts.
- Do not spam custom feeds with repetitive or near-duplicate posts.
- Avoid firehose/data use that violates user expectations or privacy-sensitive contexts.
