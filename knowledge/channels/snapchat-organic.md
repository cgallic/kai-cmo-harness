# Snapchat Organic Channel Guide

> **Use when:** Planning Snapchat Stories, Spotlight, public profile content, creator partnerships, or commercial organic content.

Primary references:
- Community Guidelines: https://values.snap.com/policy/policy-community-guidelines
- Recommendation Eligibility: https://values.snap.com/policy/content-guidelines-recommendation-eligibility
- Commercial Content Policy: https://values.snap.com/policy/content-guidelines-recommendation-eligibility/recommendation-eligibility/commercial-content
- Spotlight ranking help: https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/8961653169940-How-We-Rank-Content-on-Spotlight
- Snapchat organic rules: `harness/references/snapchat-organic-posting-rules.md`

## Channel Role

Use Snapchat for mobile-native creator content, youth-skewing brand moments, behind-the-scenes updates, Spotlight entertainment, and creator/partner distribution.

## Distribution Notes

- Allowed content and recommendation-eligible content are different checks.
- Spotlight ranking looks for viewer interest signals and negative skip behavior.
- Public content needs fast visual comprehension, vertical framing, audio/caption clarity, and originality.
- Commercial content must be disclosed and may require age/location controls.

## Guardrails

- Load `snapchat-organic-posting-rules.md`.
- Check recommendation eligibility before optimizing for non-follower reach.
- Use paid partnership and commercial disclosure tools when applicable.
- Do not push regulated goods unless eligibility and targeting controls are confirmed.
