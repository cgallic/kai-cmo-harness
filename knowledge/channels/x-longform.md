# X Longform

> **Use when:** Writing or repurposing long-form X posts, premium long posts, essay-style posts, or long threads for founders, brands, operators, and analysts.

---

## Channel Role

X longform is for public thinking with receipts.

Use it when a short post cannot carry the argument, evidence, examples, or operating lesson. Do not use it to pad a weak idea.

---

## Best-Fit Formats

| Format | Use When |
|--------|----------|
| Field note | Sharing what changed after a real campaign, audit, or experiment |
| Teardown | Explaining why a page, offer, ad, or channel move works or fails |
| Operating memo | Turning a workflow into a repeatable system |
| Market observation | Connecting several public signals into a practical point |
| Build-in-public update | Reporting progress, limits, and next action |
| Source-backed opinion | Taking a stance with evidence and caveats |

---

## Required Source Discipline

Before drafting:

- Declare source mode.
- Load source refs for every quantitative or client-facing claim.
- Mark private data and remove it before publication.
- Use public examples only when they are sourced or approved.
- Put missing proof in `_data-gaps.md`.
- Treat simulated expert panels as internal scoring, never public authority.

Do not claim "we found," "the data shows," or "customers say" unless the source ledger supports it.

---

## Structure

```markdown
Opening claim:
Why it matters:
Evidence or observation:
Example:
Operating lesson:
Practical next step:
Caveat or limit:
Soft CTA:
```

Keep the opening specific. A strong opening names the change, the audience, or the mistake.

---

## Kai Writing Rules

- Start with the point, not throat-clearing.
- Use one idea per paragraph.
- Use short paragraphs for mobile scanning.
- Name entities before pronouns.
- Use examples after claims.
- Use numbers only when sourced.
- Avoid fake certainty.
- Avoid generic founder wisdom.
- Make the lesson operational.

---

## Approval And Mutation

X longform becomes mutation-capable when it is scheduled, posted, boosted, turned into an ad, or used in outbound.

Before that happens:

- Run the privacy sanitizer.
- Run banned-word and quality gates when the post is client-facing.
- Confirm platform policy when paid promotion is planned.
- Save a dry-run artifact with final copy, media, link, tags, and account.
- Get approval from the posting owner.

## Guardrails

- Do not disclose client data, private transcripts, CRM notes, or internal metrics without approval.
- Do not use controversy that depends on unverified claims.
- Do not imply a customer endorsement without permission.
- Do not use fake expert authority.
- Do not schedule or post without approval.
