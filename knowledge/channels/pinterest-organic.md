# Pinterest Organic Channel Guide

> **Use when:** Creating Pins, idea Pins, boards, affiliate Pins, catalog-style content, or visual search content.

Primary references:
- Community Guidelines: https://policy.pinterest.com/en/community-guidelines
- Commercial and Branded Content Guidelines: https://policy.pinterest.com/en/commercial-and-branded-content-guidelines
- Pinterest Analytics: https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-analytics
- Pinterest API v5: https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/api/v5/
- Pinterest organic rules: `harness/references/pinterest-organic-posting-rules.md`

## Channel Role

Use Pinterest for visual discovery, planning, evergreen search traffic, shopping consideration, recipes, home, fashion, lifestyle, local inspiration, and high-intent content that benefits from a strong destination page.

## Distribution Notes

- Pinterest is search/discovery led. Pin relevance, image quality, destination quality, board relevance, and engagement matter.
- Fresh creative matters more than repeating the same Pin image.
- Keywords should appear naturally in titles, descriptions, boards, and destination pages.
- Seasonal content needs lead time. Publish before the search spike.

## Guardrails

- Load `pinterest-organic-posting-rules.md`.
- Match every Pin promise to the landing page.
- Disclose affiliate or branded relationships.
- Avoid link cloaking, irrelevant boards, mass duplicate pins, and misleading images.
